## Ingredients
- 8 oz shaved steak
- 1 carb wrap
- ½ onion, thinly sliced
- 1–2 slices or ¼–½ cup shredded cheddar
- 1 tbsp butter or neutral oil
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Garlic powder
- Optional: Worcestershire sauce, mayo, mustard, hot sauce

## Instructions

### 1. Cook the onions
- Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
- Add butter or oil.
- Cook the onions for 5–7 minutes until softened and lightly browned.

### 2. Cook the steak
- Push the onions to one side.
- Add the shaved steak in a single layer.
- Let it brown for about 1 minute before breaking it up.
- Season with:
  - ½ tsp garlic powder
  - ½ tsp black pepper
  - ¼ tsp salt (adjust if using Worcestershire)
- Mix with the onions.
- Optional: Add 1–2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce and cook another 30 seconds.

### 3. Melt the cheese
- Place the cheddar directly on top of the hot steak.
- Cover the pan for 30–60 seconds until melted.

### 4. Assemble
- Warm the carb wrap for 10–15 seconds if needed.
- Spoon the steak and cheese mixture into the center.
- Fold in the sides and roll it tightly like a burrito.

### 5. Toast the wrap
- Place seam-side down in the dry skillet.
- Toast 1–2 minutes per side until golden and crispy.

## Optional upgrades
- Sautéed mushrooms
- Green peppers
- Banana peppers
- Jalapeños
- A thin spread of mayo or chipotle mayo inside the wrap
- A few dashes of hot sauce

This should take about **15 minutes total** and makes a satisfying, high-protein dinner with a crisp exterior and gooey cheese.
