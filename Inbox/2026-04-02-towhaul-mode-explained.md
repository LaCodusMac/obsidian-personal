**ChatGPT:** That button with the little trailer icon is your **Tow/Haul mode**.

### What it does:
- Changes your transmission behavior for towing or carrying heavy loads  
- Holds gears longer (more power, less shifting)  
- Helps with engine braking when slowing down (especially downhill)  

### When to use it:
- Towing a trailer  
- Hauling heavy stuff (moving, loaded truck/van)

### When NOT to use it:
- Normal driving (it’ll feel sluggish and use more gas)

The sticker right below it is basically reminding you:
> Turn Tow/Haul **OFF** when you’re not towing (especially on ice/snow)
