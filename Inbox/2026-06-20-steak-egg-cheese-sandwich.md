### Quick Steak, Egg & Cheese

**Ingredients**
- Petite sirloin steak
- 2 eggs
- 2 slices cheddar (or whatever cheese you have)
- Roll, bagel, English muffin, or toast
- Salt and pepper
- Butter or a little cooking oil

### Directions
1. **Cook the steak**
   - Pat dry and season with salt and pepper.
   - Cook in a hot pan 3–4 minutes per side for medium, depending on thickness.
   - Rest 5 minutes, then slice thinly.

2. **Cook the eggs**
   - Scramble 2 eggs or fry them over-medium.
   - Season lightly.

3. **Melt the cheese**
   - Put cheese on the hot eggs or steak for 30 seconds so it melts.

4. **Toast the bread**
   - Butter and toast your roll, bagel, or bread.

5. **Assemble**
   - Bread → steak → egg → cheese.

### If you have leftover bacon
Add bacon and it becomes a serious breakfast sandwich.

### Side idea
Since you've made bacon cheese fries before, a small side of home fries or hash browns would pair much better than rice for this meal.

For a hot Saturday morning, I'd also pair it with iced coffee rather than beer or wine.

### Leftover Steak, Egg & Cheese

1. Slice the leftover petite sirloin as thinly as possible.
2. Warm it in a skillet over medium heat for 1–2 minutes. Don't overcook it or it'll get tough.
3. Cook 2 eggs:
   - Scrambled for a classic sandwich, or
   - Fried over-medium if you want the yolk slightly runny.
4. Add cheddar cheese to the eggs or directly onto the steak and let it melt.
5. Toast your bread, English muffin, bagel, or roll.
6. Assemble:
   - Steak
   - Egg
   - Cheese

If you don't have sandwich bread, steak, eggs, and cheese on a plate is still a solid high-protein breakfast.

With half a petite sirloin, I'd use:
- 2 eggs
- 1–2 slices of cheese
- Enough steak to cover the sandwich

That should be around 35–45g of protein depending on the size of the steak.

### Steak, Egg & Cheese Wrap

**Use:**
- Half leftover petite sirloin, sliced thin
- 2 eggs
- 1–2 slices cheddar cheese
- 1 carb wrap

**Steps**
1. Warm the sliced steak in a pan for 1–2 minutes.
2. Scramble 2 eggs in the same pan.
3. Add the cheese and let it melt into the eggs and steak.
4. Warm the carb wrap for about 15–20 seconds in the microwave or a dry pan.
5. Put everything in the wrap and roll it tightly.
6. Optional: Put the wrapped burrito seam-side down in the pan for 1 minute to crisp it up.

For extra flavor, add:
- Hot sauce
- Black pepper
- A little ketchup
- Sriracha

Given what you have on hand, this is probably a better meal than making rice. You'll get a high-protein breakfast/lunch with roughly:
- 40–50g protein
- Low to moderate carbs (depending on the wrap)
- Good satiety without feeling heavy.
