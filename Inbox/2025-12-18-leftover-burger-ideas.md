**You:** What to do with two left over burgers

**ChatGPT:** Two leftover burgers are actually clutch. Here are some **easy, good options** depending on how hungry / motivated you are:

### 🔥 Quick & Low-Effort
- **Burger quesadilla**  
  Chop the patties, toss in a pan with a little onion or hot sauce, throw into a tortilla with cheese.
- **Burger & eggs**  
  Crumble the burger, reheat, add scrambled or fried eggs. Great breakfast or late-night food.
- **Burger grilled cheese**  
  Slice the patties thin, sandwich them between cheese on bread. Butter + pan = elite.

### 🍚 Meal-Style
- **Burger rice bowl**  
  Chop burger, serve over rice with cheese, pickles, ketchup/mustard or burger sauce.
- **Burger pasta**  
  Crumble into cooked pasta with marinara or even a creamy sauce—basically instant beef upgrade.

### 🌮 Remix It
- **Burger tacos**  
  Chop, season lightly (salt, pepper, cumin), throw in tortillas with cheese + lettuce.
- **Burger sliders**  
  Cut patties in half, reheat, put on small buns with fresh toppings.

### 🥗 If you want “health-ish”
- **Burger salad**  
  Chopped burger over greens with tomatoes, onion, cheese, and a light dressing or burger sauce drizzle.
- **Protein plate**  
  Burger + rice or potatoes + veggie. Simple and solid.
