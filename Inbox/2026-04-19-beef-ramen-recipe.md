## 🍜 Beef Ramen (Restaurant-Style at Home)

image_group{"aspect_ratio":"1:1","query":["beef ramen bowl close up","ramen with sliced beef egg green onions","homemade beef ramen broth noodles","ramen soft boiled egg chopsticks"],"num_per_query":1}

### 🥩 Ingredients (1–2 servings)
- 1 pack ramen noodles (ditch the seasoning packet)
- 1/2 lb thin-sliced beef (shaved steak or ribeye works best)
- 4 cups beef broth (low sodium preferred)
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
- 1 tsp ginger (optional but worth it)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp chili oil (optional)
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 egg
- Green onions (chopped)
- Optional upgrades:
  - Mushrooms
  - Spinach or bok choy
  - Corn
  - Sriracha

---

### 🔥 Instructions

**1. Build the broth**
- In a pot: sauté garlic + ginger in a little oil (30–60 sec)
- Add beef broth, soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar
- Simmer 5–10 minutes

**2. Cook the beef**
- Toss beef slices straight into the simmering broth  
- Cook ~1–2 minutes (don’t overcook—it gets tough fast)

**3. Cook noodles**
- Add ramen noodles to the broth  
- Cook 2–3 minutes until tender

**4. Soft-boil egg (key upgrade)**
- Boil egg for ~6–7 minutes  
- Ice bath, peel, cut in half

**5. Assemble**
- Pour into bowl  
- Add egg, green onions, chili oil  
- Add any extras (mushrooms, spinach, etc.)

---

### 💡 Tips (this is what makes it 🔥)
- Use **shaved steak** (like for cheesesteaks) = super tender
- Add a splash of **soy + sesame oil at the end** for aroma
- If you want next-level: mix in a spoon of **butter** or **beef drippings**
