### Make: Loaded Beef Quesadillas

#### 1. Cook the beef
1. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the ground beef.
3. Add about half of the diced onion.
4. Season with:
   - Salt
   - Pepper
   - Garlic powder (if you have it)
   - Chili powder or taco seasoning (if you have it)

If you don't have taco seasoning, just salt, pepper, and hot sauce will still work.

5. Cook until browned and no pink remains.
6. Drain excess grease if needed.

#### 2. Build the quesadilla
On one half of a tortilla:

- Cheese
- Beef mixture
- Jalapeños
- Spoonful of salsa
- More cheese

Fold it over.

#### 3. Crisp it up
1. Melt a small amount of butter in the skillet.
2. Cook quesadilla 2–3 minutes per side.
3. Press gently with a spatula.
4. Remove when golden brown and cheese is melted.

#### 4. Make a quick topping
Mix together:

- Shredded lettuce
- Remaining onion
- Small spoon of salsa verde

Or just keep them separate and pile on top after cutting.

### Finish
Cut into triangles and top with:

- Lettuce
- Onion
- Salsa
- Hot sauce
- Extra jalapeños

### Upgrade
If you have eggs, crack 2 eggs into the beef after it's cooked and scramble them in. It turns into a cheesy beef breakfast-style quesadilla that's surprisingly good and adds protein.

With that amount of beef and cheese, you can easily make 3–4 large quesadillas and have leftovers for lunch tomorrow.
