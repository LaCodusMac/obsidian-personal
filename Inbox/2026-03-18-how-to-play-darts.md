### 🎯 Basic Rules (301 / 501 – Most Common Games)

**Goal:** Be the first to reach exactly **0 points**

**How it works:**
1. Start at **301 or 501 points**
2. Each turn = **3 darts**
3. Subtract your score from your total
4. You must **finish on a double** (very important)

**Example:**
- You have 40 points left  
- Hit **double 20 (D20)** → you win

### 🎯 Throwing Technique

**1. Stance**
- Stand sideways to the board  
- Keep your lead foot forward  
- Stay balanced (don’t lean too far)

**2. Grip**
- Hold the dart lightly with 2–3 fingers  
- Don’t squeeze too hard

**3. Aim**
- Keep your elbow up  
- Line up your eye → dart → target

**4. Throw**
- Smooth forward motion  
- Release at eye level  
- Follow through (hand pointing at target)

### 🎯 Easy Game Variations

**1. Around the Clock**
- Hit numbers **1 → 20 in order**
- Great for practice

**2. Cricket**
- Use numbers **15–20 + bull**
- Close numbers by hitting them 3 times
- Score points once opponent hasn’t closed them

### 🎯 Beginner Tips (This is where people improve fast)
- Aim for **20** first (big scoring area)  
- Focus on **accuracy over power**  
- Develop a **consistent routine**  
- Practice doubles (especially **D16, D20, D10**)

### 🎯 But there’s a catch…

**1. Deflections (most common)**
- Your first dart can block the target  
- Second dart might bounce off or get redirected  

**2. “Robin Hood” (rare but cool)**
- A dart sticks into the back of another dart  
- Looks awesome, but the second dart usually **does NOT count** (it didn’t hit the board)

**3. Tight grouping = skill**
- Pros actually aim to hit the same spot repeatedly  
- This is called **grouping**, and it’s how big scores happen
