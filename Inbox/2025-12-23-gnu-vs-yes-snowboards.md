## 🧠 Snowshoe Size Guide
Snowshoe size matters for flotation (not sinking into snow) and depends on **your weight with gear + where you’ll hike**:

| Weight w/ Gear (approx) | Snowshoe Length |
|------------------------|------------------|
| Up to ~180 lbs | ~22–25″ |
| ~180–220 lbs | ~25–30″ |
| 220 lbs+ (heavy pack or deep snow) | ~30–36″ |

For a typical adult without heavy packs walking trails, **25–30″ snowshoes** are usually a solid choice. citeturn0search1

## 🥾 Tips Before You Buy
- **Bindings:** Make sure they adjust easily with gloves — fiddly ones are annoying in cold.  
- **Traction:** For icy or steep trail sections, look for aggressive **crampons** and possible **heel lifts** to ease climbs.  
- **Terrain:** Smaller snowshoes are more agile on flat trails; larger float better in deep, fresh snow.  
- **Weight:** Lighter snowshoes make long hikes easier, especially if you’ll be out for hours.
