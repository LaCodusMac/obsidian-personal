## **Ground Turkey Taco Bowl Meal Prep**
**Servings:** 4–5  
**Time:** ~35 minutes  

### **Ingredients**
**For the turkey:**
- 1.5 lbs ground turkey (93% lean or leaner for meal prep)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 packet taco seasoning *(or 2–3 tbsp homemade)*
- 1/3 cup water or chicken broth

**For the base:**
- 2 cups uncooked rice *(white, brown, or cauliflower for low-carb)*
- 1 tsp salt

**Toppings / add-ins:**
- 1 cup corn (frozen, canned, or grilled)
- 1 cup black beans (drained & rinsed)
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese (cheddar or Mexican blend)
- 1/2 cup salsa or pico de gallo
- 1 avocado or guacamole (optional — best added fresh before eating)

---

### **Instructions**
1. **Cook the rice**  
   - Rinse rice well, then cook according to package directions with salt.  
   - For extra flavor, use chicken broth instead of water.

2. **Make the turkey filling**  
   - Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.  
   - Add diced onion and cook 3–4 min until softened.  
   - Add garlic, stir for 30 sec.  
   - Add ground turkey, breaking it up with a spoon, and cook until browned (about 6–8 min).  
   - Stir in taco seasoning + water/broth, reduce heat, and simmer 3–4 min until thickened.

3. **Assemble bowls**  
   - In meal prep containers, layer:  
     1. Rice (bottom layer)  
     2. Turkey taco meat  
     3. Corn + black beans  
     4. Tomatoes + lettuce + cheese  
   - Keep salsa & avocado separate until ready to eat to prevent sogginess.

4. **Store & reheat**  
   - Refrigerate for up to 4 days.  
   - Reheat rice/turkey/corn/beans portion in the microwave, then add fresh toppings.

---

💡 **Meal Prep Tip:** If you want a fajita-style version, sauté bell peppers & onions with a little cumin and chili powder, and add them to the bowls.

## **Homemade Taco Seasoning**
### Ingredients
- 1 tbsp chili powder  
- 1 tsp paprika (smoked is even better)  
- 1 tsp cumin  
- 1 tsp garlic powder  
- 1 tsp onion powder  
- 1/2 tsp oregano  
- 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes (optional, for heat)  
- 1 tsp salt (adjust to taste)  
- 1/2 tsp black pepper  

---

### Instructions
1. Mix all spices together in a small bowl.  
2. Brown your turkey, then sprinkle seasoning over the meat.  
3. Add **1/3 cup water or chicken broth** and simmer until the liquid reduces and coats the meat (like packet seasoning does).  

---
