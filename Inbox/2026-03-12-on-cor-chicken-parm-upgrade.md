## 🔥 Basic Cooking Instructions
1. **Preheat oven to 375°F**
2. **Remove film from tray**
3. Place tray on a **baking sheet**
4. Bake **60–70 minutes**
5. Let sit **5 minutes** before serving

*(Microwave works but oven keeps the chicken less soggy.)*

---

## ⭐ Quick Upgrade (Makes it Much Better)
Since the tray is mostly **chicken + sauce**, add a few things:

### Ingredients
- On-Cor chicken parm tray  
- **Spaghetti or penne**
- **Extra mozzarella or parmesan**
- **Garlic powder**
- **Olive oil**
- Optional: **fresh basil or red pepper flakes**

### Steps
1. Start the **On-Cor tray in the oven**.
2. With **15 minutes left**, boil **pasta** in salted water.
3. Drain pasta and toss with:
   - 1 tbsp olive oil  
   - pinch garlic powder  
   - little parmesan
4. Plate **pasta first**, then place **chicken parm on top**.
5. Sprinkle **extra mozzarella or parmesan** and broil **2–3 minutes** if you want it melted.
