## Garlic Burger Fried Rice Bowl

### Ingredients
- 1 leftover burger patty, chopped into small pieces
- 1½–2 cups cooked white rice (day-old is ideal)
- 1 egg (optional but recommended)
- 1–2 tsp butter or oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced (or ½ tsp garlic powder)
- Soy sauce (1–2 tbsp)
- Black pepper
- Green onions (optional)
- Shredded cheese (optional)

### Instructions
1. Chop the burger into bite-sized pieces.
2. Heat butter or oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Cook the garlic for about 30 seconds.
4. Add the burger pieces and cook until they're browned and slightly crispy.
5. Push everything to one side. Scramble the egg if using.
6. Add the rice, breaking up any clumps.
7. Stir everything together.
8. Add 1–2 tablespoons soy sauce and black pepper. Cook another 2–3 minutes until the rice starts to crisp.
9. Top with green onions or a little shredded cheddar if you'd like.

### Optional additions
- Buffalo sauce + ranch drizzle
- Sriracha + mayo
- BBQ sauce
- Frozen peas and carrots
- Diced onion or mushrooms
- A fried egg on top

If your burger already has cheese on it, just chop the whole thing up and toss it in—the melted cheese coats the rice and makes it extra flavorful. This takes about **10 minutes** from start to finish.
