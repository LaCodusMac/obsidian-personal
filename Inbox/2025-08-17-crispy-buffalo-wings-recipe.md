**ChatGPT:** Here’s a **crispy buffalo wing recipe** that gives you that restaurant-style crunch with the right level of saucy heat:  

---

### Ingredients
**For the wings:**
- 2 lbs chicken wings (split into drumettes and flats)  
- 2 tsp baking powder (NOT baking soda)  
- 1 tsp salt  
- 1 tsp garlic powder  
- 1 tsp paprika (optional, for color)  
- ½ tsp black pepper  

**For the buffalo sauce:**
- ½ cup Frank’s RedHot (or your preferred hot sauce)  
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter  
- 1 tsp honey (optional, for a touch of sweetness)  
- Pinch of garlic powder  

---

### Instructions

**1. Prep the wings**  
- Pat the wings **very dry** with paper towels. The drier, the crispier.  
- In a large bowl, toss wings with baking powder, salt, garlic powder, paprika, and pepper until evenly coated.  

**2. Bake (crispy method)**  
- Preheat oven to **425°F (220°C)**.  
- Place wings on a wire rack set over a baking sheet (for airflow and extra crispiness).  
- Bake for **20 minutes**, flip, then bake another **20–25 minutes** until golden and crispy.  

*(Air fryer option: Cook at **400°F for 18–20 minutes**, shaking halfway.)*  

**3. Make the sauce**  
- In a small saucepan over low heat, melt butter.  
- Stir in hot sauce, honey, and garlic powder. Mix until smooth.  

**4. Toss & serve**  
- Transfer crispy wings to a large bowl.  
- Pour buffalo sauce over and toss until coated.  
- Serve hot with ranch or blue cheese dressing and celery sticks.  

---

👉 If you want **extra-crispy**, you can broil the wings for 2–3 minutes after baking, just before tossing in sauce.
