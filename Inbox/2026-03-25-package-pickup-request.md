**You:** I brought a package to ship via ups store. I need it back, it’s only 11 am can I go get it ?

Here’s how it works:

### 🟢 If it’s only been a short time (like your case — 11 AM)
- The package is **very likely still in the store** waiting for pickup by UPS.
- UPS Stores are **independently owned and hold packages until a driver collects them later** citeturn0search8  
- So there’s a good chance it hasn’t left yet.

👉 **Go back immediately and ask for it.**
- Bring your **receipt (very important)**
- Tell them the **time you dropped it off**
- They can usually grab it from the outgoing pile

---

- Once UPS takes it, it’s much harder

- Next day = probably gone
