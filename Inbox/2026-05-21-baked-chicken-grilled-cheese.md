## Simple Baked Chicken Grilled Cheese

### Ingredients
- 2 slices sourdough, Texas toast, or thick white bread
- Cooked chicken breast (shredded or sliced)
- Cheese:
  - cheddar
  - mozzarella
  - provolone
  - pepper jack
- Butter or mayo for the bread
- Optional:
  - bacon
  - buffalo sauce
  - ranch
  - tomato
  - caramelized onions
  - jalapeños

## Quick Method
1. Preheat oven to **425°F**.
2. Butter or lightly mayo the outside of the bread.
3. Build sandwich:
   - cheese
   - chicken
   - extras
   - more cheese
4. Put on baking sheet.
5. Bake about **6–8 min per side** until golden and crispy.
6. Optional: broil 1 minute at end for extra crunch.

## Good Flavor Versions

### Buffalo Chicken
- Chicken + buffalo sauce + ranch + cheddar
- Add mozzarella for stretchiness

### BBQ Chicken
- BBQ sauce + red onion + smoked gouda or cheddar

### Garlic Parm
- Garlic butter outside
- Chicken + mozzarella + parmesan

### Jalapeño Popper Style
- Cream cheese + cheddar + jalapeños + bacon

## Best Bread Choices
- Sourdough = crispiest
- Texas toast = thick and filling
- Ciabatta = restaurant style
