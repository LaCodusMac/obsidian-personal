1. Bring 2 cups of water to a boil.

Here's a method that works well:

1. Bring **1 to 1¼ cups of water** to a boil (instead of the usual 2 cups).
2. Add the ramen and cook for about **2 minutes**, breaking it apart.
3. When there's just a couple tablespoons of water left, crack in **2 eggs**.

5. Turn off the heat and mix in **about ¾ of the seasoning packet** (you can add the rest if you like it saltier).

If you want them even drier:
- Drain the noodles when they're just tender, leaving only a tablespoon or two of cooking water in the pot.
- Return the pot to low heat, add the seasoning and eggs, and toss everything together until the noodles are coated.
