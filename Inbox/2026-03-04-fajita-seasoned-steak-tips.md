## 🌶️ Fajita Seasoning (Homemade)
Mix together:

- 1 tsp **chili powder**
- 1 tsp **cumin**
- 1 tsp **paprika** (smoked if you have it)
- ½ tsp **garlic powder**
- ½ tsp **onion powder**
- ½ tsp **salt**
- ½ tsp **black pepper**
- ¼ tsp **oregano**
- Optional: **¼ tsp cayenne** for heat

---

## 🍳 Cooking the Steak Tips

### Ingredients
- 1 lb **steak tips**
- 1 tbsp **oil** (avocado or olive)
- 1 **bell pepper**, sliced
- ½ **onion**, sliced
- Juice of **½ lime**
- Fajita seasoning (above)

---

### Instructions

1. **Season the steak**
   - Toss steak tips with the fajita seasoning and a little oil.
   - Let sit **10–20 minutes** if you can.

2. **Sear the steak**
   - Heat a **cast iron or heavy skillet** on high.
   - Add steak tips in a single layer.
   - Cook **2–3 minutes per side** until browned.

3. **Cook veggies**
   - Remove steak temporarily.
   - Add peppers and onions to the pan.
   - Cook **3–4 minutes** until slightly charred.

4. **Finish**
   - Add steak back in.
   - Squeeze **lime juice** over everything.
   - Toss together for **1 minute**.
