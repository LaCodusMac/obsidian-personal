## 🌯 Ground Beef Burrito (10–15 min)

### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef  
- 1 packet taco seasoning *(or chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, salt)*  
- 1/4 cup water  
- Tortillas (burrito size)  
- Cooked or canned beans  
- Shredded cheese  
- Optional: rice, sour cream, salsa, hot sauce

---

## 1️⃣ Cook the Beef
1. Heat a pan **medium-high**.  
2. Add ground beef and break it up.  
3. Cook **5–7 minutes** until browned.  
4. Drain some grease if needed.  
5. Add:
   - taco seasoning  
   - **1/4 cup water**

Simmer **2 minutes** until thick.

---

## 2️⃣ Reheat the Beans (3 easy ways)

**Microwave (fastest)**  
- Put beans in bowl  
- Add **1–2 tbsp water**  
- Cover loosely  
- Heat **1–2 minutes**, stir halfway

**Pan (better flavor)**  
- Medium heat  
- Add beans + splash of water  
- Stir **3–4 minutes**

**Pro tip:**  
Add a **pinch of cumin or chili powder** while reheating.

---

## 3️⃣ Warm the Tortilla
Heat tortilla **15–20 sec in microwave** or **10 sec per side in a pan**.

This stops it from tearing.

---

## 4️⃣ Build the Burrito
Layer in the center:

1. Beans  
2. Beef  
3. Cheese  
4. Rice *(optional)*  
5. Salsa / sour cream

Fold **bottom → sides → roll tight**.

---

🔥 **Optional upgrade (worth it):**  
Put the wrapped burrito **back in the pan seam-side down for 1–2 minutes**.  
It gets crispy like a restaurant burrito.

## Reheating Beans in the Saucepan

1. Turn the stove to **medium-low heat**.  
2. Let the beans warm **5–7 minutes**.  
3. **Stir every minute or so** so they don’t stick to the bottom.  

### If they look too watery
- Let them **simmer uncovered** for a few minutes so the liquid reduces.

### If they get too thick
- Add a **small splash of water** and stir.

### Quick flavor boost (optional)
While they heat, add:
- pinch **cumin**
- pinch **chili powder**
- small **dash of salt**
- optional **tiny bit of garlic powder**

Makes canned beans taste way better.

### When they’re done
They should be:
- **hot all the way through**
- **lightly bubbling**

Then you can spoon them straight into your burrito with the ground beef.

🔥 **Pro burrito move:**  
After you wrap it, put the burrito **seam-side down in the pan for ~1 minute** to toast the tortilla.

## 2️⃣ Cook the Ground Beef & Onions
