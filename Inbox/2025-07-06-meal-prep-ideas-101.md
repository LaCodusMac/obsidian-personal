### 🍜 **Soy-Ginger Chicken Rice Bowl** (4–5 servings)

#### **🔹 Ingredients**

**Protein:**
- 1.5–2 lbs boneless chicken thighs or breast, sliced

**Marinade/Sauce:**
- 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp sesame oil  
- 1 tbsp honey or brown sugar  
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lime juice  
- 1–2 cloves garlic, minced  
- 1 tsp fresh grated ginger (or 1/2 tsp ground)  
- Optional: chili flakes or Sriracha for heat

**Veggies:**
- 1 cup shredded carrots  
- 2 cups broccoli florets (lightly steamed or roasted)  
- 1 cup edamame (frozen is fine, just steam/microwave)  
- Optional: red bell pepper, snap peas, green onions

**Base:**
- 2 cups cooked jasmine rice or brown rice  
  (or sub cauliflower rice or quinoa if preferred)

**Toppings (optional but great):**
- Sesame seeds  
- Sriracha or chili crisp  
- Crushed peanuts  
- Cilantro or scallions

---

### 🔹 **Instructions**

1. **Marinate the chicken**  
   - Mix all marinade ingredients in a bowl.  
   - Add sliced chicken and marinate at least 30 min (or overnight for best flavor).

2. **Cook chicken**  
   - Sear in a hot skillet until cooked through and caramelized, 5–7 min.  
   - Let rest, then slice or chop.

3. **Cook or prep veggies**  
   - Lightly steam broccoli and edamame, or roast broccoli with olive oil/salt at 400°F for ~15 min.  
   - Keep carrots raw for crunch, or sauté briefly.

4. **Assemble bowls**  
   - Rice → chicken → veggies → drizzle extra sauce → add toppings

5. **Store**  
   - Refrigerate in airtight containers. Keeps 4 days.  
   - If using sauce, store separately to keep rice from getting soggy.

### 🍲 **Chicken Stir Fry Meal Prep** (4–5 servings)

#### 🔹 **Ingredients**

**Protein:**
- 1.5–2 lbs boneless chicken breast or thighs, thinly sliced

**Stir Fry Veggies:**
Use a fresh mix or frozen stir-fry blend:
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced  
- 1 cup broccoli florets  
- 1 cup snap peas or green beans  
- 1 cup shredded carrots  
- 1 small onion, sliced  
- (Or just use a 1–2 lb frozen stir-fry mix)

**Sauce:**
- 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce or hoisin (optional for sweetness)  
- 1 tbsp sesame oil  
- 1 tbsp cornstarch + 3 tbsp water (to thicken)  
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lime juice  
- 1–2 tsp honey or brown sugar  
- 1 clove garlic, minced  
- 1 tsp grated ginger (or 1/2 tsp ground)  
- Optional: red pepper flakes or Sriracha for heat

**Base:**
- 2 cups uncooked jasmine or brown rice (yields ~4–5 cups cooked)  
  *(Optional: sub noodles or cauliflower rice)*

---

### 🔹 **Instructions**

1. **Cook rice**  
   - Start your rice first so it's ready when the stir fry is done.

2. **Prep sauce**  
   - Mix all sauce ingredients together in a bowl (except cornstarch and water — add that last, when cooking).

3. **Cook chicken**  
   - Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan or wok.  
   - Add chicken, season with salt/pepper, cook until browned and cooked through. Remove and set aside.

4. **Cook veggies**  
   - In the same pan, add a little more oil if needed. Stir fry veggies for ~5–6 minutes until tender-crisp.

5. **Add chicken back + sauce**  
   - Add chicken back in with the veggies.  
   - Pour in sauce. Once it bubbles, add the cornstarch slurry (cornstarch mixed with water) and stir until thickened.

6. **Assemble bowls**  
   - Rice → stir fry mixture → top with sesame seeds, green onion, or chili flakes

7. **Store**  
   - Divide into airtight containers.  
   - Keeps 4–5 days in the fridge. Reheat in microwave or skillet.

---

### 🔹 **Variations**
- Swap chicken for beef strips, shrimp, or tofu  
- Use soba or rice noodles instead of rice  
- Make it spicy with chili oil or Gochujang

## 🍲 **Easy Chicken Stir Fry Rice Bowls**  
**Makes 5 meals (lunch or dinner for the week)**

---

### 🔹 **Ingredients**

**Protein:**
- 1.5–2 lbs boneless chicken breast or thighs, diced

**Veggies (fresh or frozen):**
- 3–4 cups total — a mix of:  
  - Broccoli  
  - Bell peppers  
  - Carrots  
  - Snap peas  
  - Onion (optional)

**Base:**
- 2 cups uncooked jasmine or brown rice  
  → Yields ~6 cups cooked

**Sauce (mix in a bowl):**
- 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp sesame oil  
- 1 tbsp honey or brown sugar  
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lime juice  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced  
- 1 tsp grated ginger (or 1/2 tsp ground)  
- 1 tbsp cornstarch + 3 tbsp water (for thickening)

---

### 🔹 **Instructions**

1. **Cook rice**  
   - Make all the rice at once. Let it cool before portioning.

2. **Cook chicken**  
   - Heat a bit of oil in a large skillet or wok.  
   - Season chicken lightly with salt/pepper. Cook until golden and done. Remove and set aside.

3. **Cook veggies**  
   - In the same pan, cook your veggies until tender but still a bit crisp (~5–7 min).

4. **Add chicken back + sauce**  
   - Pour sauce over chicken + veggies in the pan.  
   - Once it starts bubbling, stir in the cornstarch slurry. Cook 1–2 more minutes until thickened.

5. **Assemble bowls**  
   - Rice + chicken stir fry.  
   - Add optional toppings: sesame seeds, chili flakes, green onion

6. **Store**  
   - Let everything cool. Portion into 5 containers.  
   - Fridge: Up to 4–5 days  
   - Freezer: Up to 2 months (but veggies may lose crunch)

---

### 🔁 Optional Tweaks
- Swap chicken for ground turkey or tofu  
- Add egg for extra protein (fry or scramble)  
- Use cauliflower rice for low-carb version  
- Spice it up with sriracha or chili crisp

## 🥜 **Thai-Inspired Peanut Chicken Rice Bowl**  
**5 meals for the week — creamy, sweet, and savory**

---

### 🔹 **Ingredients**

**Protein:**
- 1.5–2 lbs boneless chicken breast or thighs, cubed

**Veggies:**
- 2 cups shredded carrots  
- 2 cups red cabbage (shredded or chopped)  
- 1–2 cups edamame (shelled, frozen is fine)  
- 1 bell pepper (optional)

**Base:**
- 2 cups uncooked jasmine or brown rice  
  → ~6 cups cooked

**Peanut Sauce:**
- 1/3 cup peanut butter (creamy, natural preferred)  
- 2 tbsp soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup  
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lime juice  
- 1 tsp sesame oil  
- 1–2 tbsp warm water (to thin)  
- Optional: 1 tsp sriracha or chili flakes for heat

**Toppings (optional):**
- Crushed peanuts  
- Cilantro  
- Green onions  
- Lime wedges

---

### 🔹 **Instructions**

1. **Cook rice**  
   - Make it all at once and let cool before portioning.

2. **Cook chicken**  
   - Sear diced chicken in a pan with a little oil, salt, pepper.  
   - Cook until browned and done (~6–8 min). Set aside.

3. **Make peanut sauce**  
   - Mix all sauce ingredients in a bowl until smooth. Thin with warm water until pourable.

4. **Prep veggies**  
   - Steam or microwave edamame.  
   - Use shredded carrots and cabbage raw for crunch (or sauté briefly if preferred).

5. **Assemble bowls**  
   - Rice + chicken + veggies + drizzle of peanut sauce  
   - Add optional toppings if using

6. **Store**  
   - Let cool, then portion into 5 airtight containers.  
   - Add sauce fresh if you want it extra creamy, or mix it in while still warm.

---

### 🔁 Why it’s great:
- No special tools or baking required  
- Flavors hold up all week  
- Tastes good cold or hot  
- Vegetarian? Swap chicken for tofu or chickpeas
