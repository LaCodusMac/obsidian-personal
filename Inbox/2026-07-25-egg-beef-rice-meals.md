### Leftover Beef & Egg Fried Rice (10 minutes)

**Ingredients**
- Leftover cooked beef, chopped or sliced
- Leftover cooked rice
- 1–2 eggs
- 1–2 tbsp soy sauce (if you have it)
- Butter or oil
- Black pepper
- Garlic or onion (optional)

**Instructions**
1. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with a little oil or butter.
2. Add the beef and cook for 1–2 minutes until warmed through. Remove it if you don't want it to overcook.
3. Crack the egg into the pan and scramble it until just set.
4. Add the rice, breaking up any clumps. Cook for 3–4 minutes so it gets hot and slightly crispy.
5. Stir in the beef, soy sauce, and black pepper. If you have garlic powder, onion powder, or a pinch of paprika, add those too.
6. Cook for another minute and serve.

If you like a runny yolk, instead of scrambling the egg, fry it separately and place it on top of the rice. Break the yolk over everything just before eating—it makes the dish much richer.

## Asian-Inspired Spicy Beef Fried Rice

**Ingredients**
- Leftover ground beef with onions and jalapeños
- Cold rice
- 1–2 eggs
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil (if you have it)
- 1 tsp sriracha or chili garlic sauce
- 1 tsp minced garlic (or ½ tsp garlic powder)
- Green onions (optional)

### Directions
1. Heat a skillet until very hot.
2. Add the beef and cook for 2 minutes.
3. Push it to one side and scramble the eggs.
4. Add the rice and let it crisp for 2–3 minutes.
5. Stir in:
   - 2 tbsp soy sauce
   - 1 tsp sesame oil
   - Sriracha to taste
   - Garlic
6. Mix everything together and cook another minute.
7. Top with green onions and a fried egg if you want.
