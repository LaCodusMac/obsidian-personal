### Steak Tips
**Ingredients**
- 1 lb beef sirloin steak tips
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Garlic powder
- Optional: paprika, onion powder

**Season**
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp paprika

### Cook
1. Pat steak tips dry.
2. Toss with oil and seasoning.
3. Heat a cast iron or heavy skillet on high until very hot.
4. Add steak tips in a single layer.
5. Cook about:
   - Rare: 2-3 min per side
   - Medium-rare: 3-4 min per side
   - Medium: 4-5 min per side
6. Remove and rest 5 minutes.

### Rice & Beans
If the rice is already cooked:
1. Heat a pan with a little oil or butter.

3. Season with:
   - Garlic powder
   - Onion powder
   - Pinch of cumin (if you have it)
   - Salt and pepper
4. Stir in the rice and cook 3-5 minutes.

### Steak Tips
