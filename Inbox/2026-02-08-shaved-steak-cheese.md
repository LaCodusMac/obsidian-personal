**ChatGPT:** Here’s a **classic shaved steak & cheese** that’s quick and hits every time 🔥

### Ingredients
- Shaved steak (8–12 oz)
- Hoagie rolls or sub rolls  
- Provolone or American cheese (or Cheez Whiz if you want Philly-style)
- Onion (optional)
- Bell peppers or mushrooms (optional)
- Oil or butter
- Salt, black pepper  
- Optional: garlic powder, Worcestershire sauce

### How to Make It
1. **Heat the pan**
   - Cast iron or skillet on **medium-high**
   - Add a little oil or butter

2. **Cook onions/peppers (optional)**
   - Sauté 3–5 minutes until soft
   - Push to the side or remove

3. **Cook the shaved steak**
   - Add steak in an even layer
   - Season with salt & pepper
   - Let it sear for ~30 seconds, then flip and break it up
   - Add a splash of Worcestershire if you want extra flavor

4. **Combine**
   - Mix steak with onions/peppers
   - Shape into sandwich-sized piles

5. **Melt the cheese**
   - Lay cheese over the steak
   - Cover pan for 30–60 seconds until melted

6. **Toast the bread**
   - Optional but recommended: butter the roll and toast cut-side down

7. **Assemble & serve**
   - Scoop steak + cheese into the roll

### Quick Variations
- **Steak melt**: Use rye or Texas toast
- **Garlic cheesesteak**: Add garlic butter to the bread
- **Spicy**: Add banana peppers or a dash of hot sauce
- **Quesadilla style**: Toss the steak & cheese in a tortilla (you mentioned you’ve done that before 👀)

**ChatGPT:** Here are the **best ways to heat a hoagie roll**, depending on what you want:

### 🔥 Best Overall (Oven)
1. Preheat oven to **350°F**
2. Split the hoagie (don’t cut all the way through)
3. Wrap loosely in foil **or** place cut-side up
4. Heat **5–8 minutes**  
   - Unwrapped = lightly toasted  
   - Wrapped = soft & warm

### 🍳 Quick & Tasty (Stovetop)
1. Heat a pan on **medium**
2. Lightly butter the cut sides
3. Place cut-side down
4. Toast **1–2 minutes** until golden

### ⚡ Fastest (Microwave – soft only)
1. Wrap roll in a **slightly damp paper towel**
2. Microwave **10–15 seconds**
3. Good if you want it soft, not crispy

### 🧀 Cheesesteak Tip
- Toast **just the inside**, keep the outside soft so it doesn’t tear
- If assembling in the pan, you can lay the roll over the steak & cheese for **30 seconds** to steam it slightly
