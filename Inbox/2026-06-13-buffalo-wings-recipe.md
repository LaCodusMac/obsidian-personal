### Ingredients
- 2–3 lbs chicken wings (split flats/drums)
- 1 tbsp baking powder (not baking soda)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp black pepper

### Buffalo Sauce
- ½ cup hot sauce (classic: entity["food","Frank's RedHot Original Cayenne Pepper Sauce","hot sauce"])
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Optional: 1 tsp honey for a slightly smoother sauce

### Oven Method (Best if no fryer)
1. Pat wings completely dry with paper towels.
2. Toss with baking powder, salt, garlic powder, paprika, and pepper.
3. Place on a wire rack over a baking sheet.
4. Refrigerate uncovered for 1–8 hours if you have time (improves crispiness).
5. Bake at **425°F (220°C)**:
   - 25 minutes
   - Flip
   - Another 20–25 minutes
6. Wings should be deeply golden and crispy.

### Air Fryer Method
1. Prep wings the same way.
2. Air fry at **400°F** for 20–24 minutes.
3. Shake halfway through.

### Sauce
1. Melt butter.
2. Stir in hot sauce, garlic powder, and honey (if using).
3. Toss hot wings immediately in sauce.

### If You Want Restaurant-Style Buffalo Wings
After saucing:
- Put them back in the oven or air fryer for **2–3 minutes**.
- This helps the sauce adhere and creates that wing-joint texture.
