# 🔥 **Easy Fried Rice (Restaurant Style)**  
**Serves:** 2–3  
**Time:** 15 minutes  

## ✅ **Ingredients**
- 2 cups **cold cooked rice** (day-old is best)
- 2 tbsp **oil** (vegetable, canola, or sesame mix)
- 2 **eggs**, beaten  
- 1/2 cup **frozen peas & carrots** (or any veg)
- 1/4 cup **onion**, diced (optional but recommended)
- 1–2 cloves **garlic**, minced  
- 2–3 tbsp **soy sauce**
- 1 tsp **sesame oil** (optional but makes it legit)
- Salt + pepper  
- Protein (optional):  
  - Cooked chicken/beef/shrimp, or  
  - 1/4 lb raw chicken/beef cut small  

---

## 🔪 **Instructions**

### 1️⃣ Prep (Important)
- Break up your cold rice with your hands or a spoon so it’s loose.  
- If using raw meat, season lightly with salt/pepper.

---

### 2️⃣ Stir-Fry  
**High heat all the way.**

1. **Heat oil** in a big pan or wok until shimmering.  
2. Add **onions** → 1 min.  
3. Add **garlic** → 15–20 sec (don’t burn it).  
4. If using **raw protein**, cook it now until done.  
   If it’s cooked already, wait until the end.

---

### 3️⃣ Eggs  
5. Push everything to the side.  
6. Add a little oil, pour in **beaten eggs**, scramble until almost set.

---

### 4️⃣ Rice + Veggies  
7. Add your **cold rice**, **peas/carrots**, and mix it all together.  
8. Add **soy sauce**, working it around so every part gets some.  
9. Add **sesame oil** and black pepper.  
10. Stir-fry 2–3 more minutes until everything smells amazing.

---

### 5️⃣ Finish
- Add cooked meat back in (if using).  
- Taste → add more soy sauce if needed.  
- Optional: a squeeze of **butter** for hibachi-style flavor.

---

# 🔥 Pro Tips
- **Cold rice only.** Fresh rice turns mushy.  
- **Butter + soy + sesame oil = hibachi vibes**  
- Add **green onions** at the end for perfect flavor.  
- If you want spicy, add **Sriracha** or **chili crisp**.

# 🥩🔥 **Steak Fried Rice (Hibachi Style)**  
**Serves:** 2–3  
**Time:** 20 minutes  

## ✅ **Ingredients**
**For the steak**
- 6–8 oz **steak** (sirloin, ribeye, NY strip, flank — anything works)  
- 1–2 tbsp **soy sauce**  
- 1 tsp **garlic powder** (or minced garlic)  
- Salt + pepper  
- 1–2 tsp **oil**  
- Optional: 1 tsp **butter** for finishing  

**For the fried rice**
- 2 cups **cold cooked rice** (day-old is best)  
- 2 eggs  
- 2 tbsp oil (veg/canola)  
- 1/2 cup peas & carrots  
- 1/4 cup diced onions  
- 2–3 tbsp soy sauce  
- 1 tsp sesame oil  
- 1–2 cloves garlic, minced  
- Optional: 1 tbsp **butter** (gives it that hibachi flavor)  
- Optional: green onions  

---

# 🔥 **Instructions**

## 1️⃣ Cook the Steak
1. Cut steak into **small bite-sized cubes** (or thin strips).  
2. Season with **salt, pepper, garlic powder, and soy sauce**.  
3. Heat a pan/wok on **high** with oil.  
4. Add steak and **sear** 1–2 minutes per side until browned.  
5. Remove from pan and set aside (don’t overcook — medium is perfect).  
6. Optional: add a small **pat of butter** while it’s resting.

---

## 2️⃣ Cook the Fried Rice
1. In the same pan, add a little more oil.  
2. Add **onions** → cook 1 minute.  
3. Add **garlic** → 15 seconds.  
4. Push to the side, pour **scrambled eggs** in the empty space.  
5. Scramble until just set, then mix with onions.  
6. Add **cold rice** — break it up and mix.  
7. Add **peas & carrots**.  
8. Add **soy sauce** (start with 2 tbsp), mix well.  
9. Add **sesame oil**, black pepper.  
10. Optional: add **butter** for that hibachi flavor.  

---

## 3️⃣ Add the Steak Back  
- Toss the cooked steak back in, mix everything, and taste.  
- Add more soy sauce or a splash of **teriyaki** if you like it sweeter.  

---

# 🔥 Pro Tips
- You want **high heat** the whole time for wok flavor.  
- **Don’t overcrowd the steak** or it won’t sear.  
- A tiny splash of **rice vinegar** or **mirin** at the very end adds flavor.  
- Garlic butter + steak + rice = elite combo.
