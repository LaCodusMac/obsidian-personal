# 🧠 What’s Actually Worth Printing vs Buying

### Print it:
- Custom mounts
- Cable management
- Blanks with features
- Clips/tools

### Just buy it:
- Rails (load-bearing)
- Heavy shelves
- Anything holding >10–15 lbs long-term

# ⚙️ Print Settings (quick dial-in)
- **Material:** PETG (best balance) or PLA+ (fine for light use)
- **Infill:** 20–40% (50%+ for mounts)
- **Walls:** 3–4 perimeters
- **Orientation:**  
  - Mounts → layer lines *perpendicular* to load  
  - Clips → print flat for strength
