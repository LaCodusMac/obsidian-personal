- Laundromat closes at **10:00 PM**
- Wash cycle takes about **30 minutes**
- They may post **Last Wash: 9:00 PM** or **9:30 PM**

This doesn't mean the building closes at that time—it means you can't start a new load after that point.

**You:** Oil in water

**ChatGPT:** For spaghetti water, **don't add oil to the water**.

A common myth is that oil keeps pasta from sticking, but it mostly just floats on top of the water. It can actually make the pasta slightly slippery, which makes it harder for the sauce to cling to it.

Instead:

1. Bring water to a rolling boil.
2. Add **1 tablespoon of salt**.
3. Add pasta.
4. Stir well for the first 30–60 seconds.
5. Stir once or twice during cooking.

If you're using oil at all, use a little in the pan when cooking the turkey—not in the pasta water. Ground turkey is lean, so 1 teaspoon to 1 tablespoon of oil can help prevent sticking if your pan tends to do that.
