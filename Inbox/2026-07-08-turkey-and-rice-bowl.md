### Garlic Turkey & Rice Bowl (2 servings)

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb ground turkey (93% lean works well)
- 2 cups cooked white rice
- 1 tsp oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp salt
- Optional: broccoli, peas, carrots, corn, or spinach
- Soy sauce or hot sauce to taste

### Instructions
1. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with the oil.
2. Add the turkey and break it up with a spatula.
3. Season with garlic, onion powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
4. Cook for 7–10 minutes until the turkey is fully cooked (165°F/74°C).
5. If using vegetables, add them during the last 3–5 minutes.
6. Stir in a splash of soy sauce if you'd like.
7. Serve over the cooked rice.

### Optional sauce
Mix together:
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp honey (optional)
- 1 tsp sriracha or buffalo sauce
- ½ tsp garlic powder

Pour over the turkey and rice at the end.

### Approximate nutrition (per serving)
- Calories: ~500–550
- Protein: ~38–42 g
- Carbs: ~45 g
- Fat: ~15–18 g

If you're trying to lose weight, keep the rice to about **1 cup cooked per serving** and load up on vegetables. If it's after a workout or you're bulking, increase the rice to **1½–2 cups per serving**.

### Ground Turkey Fried Rice

1. Heat pan medium-high with a little oil.
2. Add ground turkey. Break it up.
3. Season with:
   - salt
   - pepper
   - garlic powder
   - onion powder
   - paprika
4. Cook until no pink left.
5. Push turkey to side. Add 1–2 eggs if you have them. Scramble.
6. Add day-old rice. Break it up.
7. Add soy sauce, a little butter, and hot sauce/sriracha if you want.
8. Stir-fry 3–5 minutes until rice gets hot and a little crispy.

Best add-ins: frozen peas/carrots, onion, corn, spinach, or shredded cheese if you want it more “bowl” style.
