**ChatGPT:** If it’s actual eyebrow wax (like salon/hard wax) on hardwood, the safest approach is:

1. **Harden the wax first**
- Put ice in a plastic bag.
- Hold it on the wax for a few minutes until the wax gets brittle.

2. **Gently scrape**
- Use a plastic card, plastic scraper, or even your fingernail.
- Avoid metal tools — they can gouge the finish.

3. **Remove residue**
Use one of these lightly on a microfiber cloth:
- a tiny bit of mineral oil
- hardwood floor cleaner
- diluted dish soap

Rub gently with the wood grain.

4. **Dry immediately**
- Wipe with a dry cloth so moisture doesn’t sit on the wood.

Avoid:
- acetone
- Goo Gone directly soaking the wood
- magic erasers aggressively
- heat guns/hair dryers unless absolutely necessary (they can soften the floor finish)

If there’s still a cloudy or sticky spot after the wax is gone, send a close-up photo and I can tell you whether it’s leftover wax or damage to the finish.
