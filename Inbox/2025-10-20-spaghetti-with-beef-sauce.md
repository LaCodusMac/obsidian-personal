**ChatGPT:** Here’s a simple and tasty way to make spaghetti with **store-bought sauce and ground beef** — perfect for an easy weeknight meal 👇  

---

### 🍝 **Ingredients**
- 1 lb ground beef (80/20 or leaner)
- 1 jar (24 oz) of your favorite pasta sauce (e.g. Ragu, Prego, Rao’s)
- 8–12 oz spaghetti noodles
- 1 tbsp olive oil (optional)
- Salt
- Pepper
- Optional: minced garlic, onion, Italian seasoning, or crushed red pepper for extra flavor
- Optional: grated Parmesan and fresh basil to serve

---

### 🔥 **Instructions**

**1. Boil the pasta**
1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
2. Add spaghetti and cook according to the package directions (usually 9–11 minutes).
3. Drain, toss with a little olive oil to prevent sticking, and set aside.

---

**2. Brown the ground beef**
1. Heat a large skillet over **medium-high heat**.
2. Add the ground beef (and a drizzle of olive oil if it’s very lean).
3. Break it up with a spatula or spoon as it cooks.
4. Season lightly with **salt and pepper** (and optional garlic/onion/Italian seasoning).
5. Cook until browned and no pink remains — about **6–8 minutes**.
6. Drain excess grease if needed.

---

**3. Add the sauce**
1. Lower heat to **medium-low**.
2. Pour in your jar of pasta sauce.
3. Stir well and let simmer **5–10 minutes** to blend the flavors.
   - (Optional: Add a splash of water if the sauce is too thick.)

---

**4. Combine and serve**
1. Add cooked spaghetti directly into the pan and toss to coat, **or** serve sauce over the noodles.
2. Top with Parmesan and basil if desired.

---

### ✅ **Quick Tips**
- Want richer flavor? Add 1 tsp of sugar and a splash of red wine or balsamic vinegar to the sauce while simmering.
- For more texture, sauté chopped onion and garlic before adding the beef.
- Leftovers store great in the fridge up to 3–4 days.
