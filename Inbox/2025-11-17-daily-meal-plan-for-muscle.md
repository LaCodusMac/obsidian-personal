# 🍳 Beginner Cooking Tips
| Goal | Quick Tip |
|------|-----------|
| Avoid dry chicken | Cook thighs instead of breasts, air fryer 390°F ~16 min |
| Juicy ground meat | Don’t press it while cooking; let it brown |
| Perfect rice | 1 cup rice : 2 cups water, simmer covered 18 min |
| Better veggies | Roast with olive oil, salt, pepper at 400°F for 20 min |
| Consistency | Cook 6–8 portions once, reheat during week |
