## 🔥 Steak, Egg & Hash Brown Quesadilla (Top Pick)
**How to do it:**
1. Cook hash brown sticks until crispy → set aside  
2. Quick-sear shaved steak with salt, pepper, garlic powder  
3. Scramble 1–2 eggs (soft, not dry)  
4. Assemble tortilla:
   - Cheese down first  
   - Steak  
   - Eggs  
   - Hash brown sticks (break them in half)  
   - More cheese  
5. Cook low-medium heat until golden and melty

👉 Serve with hot sauce or salsa if you’ve got it.

---

## 🥔 Breakfast Steak Skillet (No Tortilla)
- Steak + eggs scrambled together  
- Hash brown sticks on the side or chopped in  
- Melt cheese over everything at the end  
Fast, filling, no mess.

---

## 🧀 Extra Crispy Tip
After flipping the quesadilla, sprinkle a little cheese **directly into the pan**, then press the quesadilla into it → crispy cheese crust 🔥
