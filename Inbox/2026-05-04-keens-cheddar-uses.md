## 🔥 Shaved Steak Sandwich (with your Keen’s Cheddar)

### 🛒 What you need
- Shaved steak (or very thin sliced beef)
- Your **Keen’s cheddar**
- Hoagie roll / sub roll (soft inside, slight crust outside)
- Onion (optional but highly recommended)
- Butter or oil
- Salt, pepper

---

## 👨‍🍳 How to make it (don’t overthink this)

### 1. Cook the onions first (if using)
- Medium heat, little oil  
- Let them get soft + slightly browned  
- Set aside

### 2. Cook the steak FAST
- High heat pan  
- Toss steak in, spread it out  
- Season with salt + pepper  
- Let it sear, then chop/mix it up  
- Takes like 2–3 minutes max

### 3. Add the cheese (key move)
- Lower heat slightly  
- Lay **thin slices** of your cheddar over the steak  
- Add onions back on top  
- Let it melt *just enough* (don’t expect gooey pull)

### 4. Toast the bread
- Butter the inside  
- Toast in a pan until golden

### 5. Build it
- Load steak + cheese into the roll  
- Press slightly so everything sticks together

---

## 🔑 Tips (this is what makes it hit)
- Slice the cheddar thin → melts way better  
- Don’t overcook steak → it gets tough fast  
- Bread matters more than you think (don’t use weak sandwich bread)  
- Want more melt? Add a little provolone or American with it (cheddar = flavor, those = melt)
