**ChatGPT:** Here’s a solid **Spanish rice (Mexican-style red rice) recipe** that’s flavorful and easy to make:

### Ingredients (4 servings)
- 1 cup **long-grain white rice** (like jasmine or basmati works, but traditional is medium/long-grain)
- 2 tbsp **oil** (vegetable, olive, or canola)
- 1/2 **medium onion**, finely diced
- 2 cloves **garlic**, minced
- 1 **Roma tomato**, diced (or 1/2 cup canned diced tomato)
- 2 cups **chicken broth** (or veggie broth for vegetarian)
- 1/4 cup **tomato sauce** (or blended tomato + a little water)
- 1 tsp **salt** (adjust to taste)
- 1/2 tsp **ground cumin**
- 1/4 tsp **chili powder** (optional, for heat)
- 1/2 cup **frozen peas and carrots** (optional, but traditional in many versions)
- Fresh **cilantro** (for garnish, optional)
- Lime wedges (optional for serving)

---

### Instructions
1. **Toast the rice**  
   - In a skillet or saucepan, heat oil over medium heat.  
   - Add the rice and stir frequently until it turns golden brown (about 5 minutes). This gives the rice a nutty flavor.

2. **Cook aromatics**  
   - Stir in onion and garlic, cook until softened and fragrant (2–3 minutes).

3. **Add tomato + seasoning**  
   - Stir in diced tomato (or tomato sauce), cumin, chili powder, and salt. Cook for another minute.

4. **Simmer**  
   - Pour in broth and stir. Bring to a gentle boil.  
   - Reduce heat to low, cover with a lid, and simmer 18–20 minutes (don’t lift the lid).  

5. **Finish**  
   - Remove from heat, keep covered for 5 minutes.  
   - Fluff with a fork, stir in peas & carrots if using, garnish with cilantro, and serve.  

---

🔥 **Pro Tips**  
- For **extra flavor**, blend the tomato, onion, garlic, and a bit of broth into a smooth sauce before cooking instead of just dicing.  
- If you want it more **authentic restaurant-style**, use Knorr **chicken bouillon powder** instead of plain salt.  
- Works great as a side for tacos, enchiladas, grilled chicken, or steak fajitas.
