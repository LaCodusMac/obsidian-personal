**You:** How to cook shredded hash browns

**ChatGPT:** Here’s a simple and foolproof way to cook **crispy shredded hash browns** — whether they’re fresh or frozen:

---

### 🔥 **Ingredients**
- 2 cups **shredded potatoes** (fresh or frozen and thawed)
- 2 tbsp **oil or butter** (vegetable oil, canola, or clarified butter like ghee works best)
- Salt and pepper to taste  
- Optional: onion powder, garlic powder, paprika

---

### 🍳 **Stovetop Method (Pan-Fried Crispy)**
1. **Prep the potatoes** (if fresh):
   - Rinse shredded potatoes in cold water to remove excess starch.
   - Squeeze out all moisture using a towel or cheesecloth (super important for crispiness).
   - If using frozen, thaw and squeeze dry.

2. **Heat a pan**:
   - Use a **non-stick or cast-iron skillet** over medium-high heat.
   - Add **2 tbsp oil or butter** and let it get hot.

3. **Spread the potatoes**:
   - Add shredded potatoes in a thin, even layer.
   - Press down slightly with a spatula.
   - Don’t stir! Let them **crisp up** — about **5–7 minutes**.

4. **Flip carefully**:
   - Use a spatula to flip in sections, or slide the whole thing onto a plate and invert back into the pan.
   - Cook another **5–7 minutes** until golden brown and crispy.

5. **Season and serve**:
   - Salt, pepper, and any spices you like.
   - Serve hot!

---

### 💡 Tips for Perfect Hash Browns
- The **drier the potato**, the crispier the result.
- Don't crowd the pan — cook in batches if needed.
- Use **high-smoke point oils** (like avocado or canola) for best browning.
- Want extra crunch? Add a little flour (1 tsp) to the shredded potatoes.
