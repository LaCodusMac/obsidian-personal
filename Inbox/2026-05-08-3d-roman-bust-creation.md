## Easiest Method (Best Balance of Quality + Simplicity)

### Option 1 — Phone Photos → AI/Photogrammetry → Roman Bust

### You’ll need:
- Your iPhone
- Good lighting
- A turn-around set of photos/video
- A PC/Mac for cleanup/slicing

## Step 1: Capture Your Face

Take:
- 30–80 photos around your head
OR
- Slow 360° video around your face

Tips:
- Bright diffuse lighting
- Neutral background
- Don’t smile/move
- Keep hair simple if possible
- Include side and back angles

## Ideal Print Size for Your Printer

For the entity["product","Prusa Mini"]:
- 120–150mm tall bust = easiest
- Full shoulders + chest bust should still fit

If larger:
- Split model into pieces in PrusaSlicer or Blender

## Pro Tip
Roman busts look BETTER with imperfections:
- Slight layer lines
- Sanded nose/edges
- Matte spray finish
- Small “broken marble” effect

It hides scan imperfections really well.
