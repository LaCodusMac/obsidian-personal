# 🔌 How to Connect to Your AVR (super simple)

## What you need:
- Speaker wire (16 gauge is perfect)

## Steps:

1. **Cut wire (2 equal lengths)**
2. Strip ends (~1/2 inch)

3. On your AVR:
   - Find **Front Left / Front Right**
   - Red = positive (+)  
   - Black = negative (–)

4. On speakers:
   - Same thing → red to red, black to black

👉 Example flow:
```
AVR Front Left  → Left speaker  
AVR Front Right → Right speaker
```

⚠️ Don’t mix polarity or it’ll sound weird (weak bass)
