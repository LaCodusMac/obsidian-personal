## 🌍 What causes a geomagnetic storm?
entity["scientific_concept","Geomagnetic storm","disturbance of Earth's magnetosphere"]  

A geomagnetic storm happens when:
1. The Sun ejects plasma (a CME)
2. It travels toward Earth
3. It slams into Earth’s magnetic field (the magnetosphere)

This can:
- Disrupt satellites  
- Affect GPS and radio  
- Create **auroras (northern lights)** much farther south than usual
