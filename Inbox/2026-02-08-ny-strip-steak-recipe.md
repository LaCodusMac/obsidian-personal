## What you need
- NY strip steak (1–1¼ inch thick is ideal)
- Salt & black pepper  
- High-smoke oil (avocado, canola, or vegetable)
- Butter (optional but recommended)
- Garlic cloves & fresh rosemary or thyme (optional)

---

## Step-by-step

### 1️⃣ Bring steak to room temp  
Take it out of the fridge **30 minutes before cooking**.  
Pat completely dry with paper towels.

### 2️⃣ Season generously  
Season **both sides** with salt and pepper right before cooking.

---

### 3️⃣ Heat the pan  
- Use a **cast iron or heavy stainless steel pan**
- Heat on **medium-high** for 3–5 minutes  
- Add **1 tbsp oil** — it should shimmer/smoke lightly

---

### 4️⃣ Sear the steak  
Place steak in the pan **away from you**.

**Do NOT move it.**

- 1-inch steak:
  - **3–4 min first side**
  - Flip, then **2–3 min second side**

---

### 5️⃣ Butter baste (optional but 🔥)
Lower heat to **medium**.
Add:
- 1–2 tbsp butter  
- Garlic + herbs  

Tilt pan and spoon butter over steak for **30–60 seconds**.

---

### 6️⃣ Check doneness  
Use a thermometer if possible:
- Rare: **120–125°F**
- Medium-rare: **130–135°F** ✅ best for NY strip
- Medium: **140–145°F**

---

### 7️⃣ Rest  
Remove steak and **rest 5 minutes** before slicing.

---

## Pro tips
- Don’t overcrowd the pan
- Flip only once
- If steak is thicker than 1¼", finish in oven at **400°F for 3–5 min**
- Slice **against the grain**
