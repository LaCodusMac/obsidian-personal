Here’s a basic crispy oven-baked version to get you started:  

**Ingredients**  
- 2 lbs chicken wings (flats + drums)  
- 1 tbsp baking powder (not baking soda)  
- 1 tsp salt  
- 1 tsp garlic powder  
- ½ tsp black pepper  
- Optional: paprika or cayenne for extra kick  

**Steps**  
1. **Dry the wings well** with paper towels (crispy secret).  
2. Toss wings in baking powder, salt, pepper, and garlic powder.  
3. Arrange on a wire rack over a baking sheet.  
4. Bake at **425°F for 40–50 min**, flipping halfway.  
5. Toss in your sauce (buffalo, BBQ, honey garlic, etc.) right before serving.

**Buffalo Sauce (for ~2 lbs wings)**  
- ½ cup Frank’s RedHot (or similar cayenne hot sauce)  
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter (½ stick)  
- 1 tsp garlic powder  
- ½ tsp onion powder  
- Optional: ½–1 tsp honey (for a touch of sweetness)  

**Steps**  
1. Melt butter in a saucepan over low heat.  
2. Stir in hot sauce, garlic powder, and onion powder until smooth.  
3. Taste—add a little honey if you like a sweeter, less sharp sauce.  
4. Toss your baked/fried wings in the sauce while they’re still hot.  

🔥 Pro tip: If you want **extra spicy buffalo**, add a splash of cayenne or Tabasco.
