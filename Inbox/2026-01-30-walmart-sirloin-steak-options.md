### Simple Steak Sandwich (10–15 min)

**Ingredients**
- Great Value Sirloin Philly Steak (frozen or thawed)
- Hoagie roll or sub roll
- Onion (sliced)
- Bell pepper (optional)
- Cheese: provolone, American, or mozzarella
- Oil or butter
- Salt & pepper  
- Optional: garlic powder, Worcestershire, mayo

**Steps**
1. **Toast the roll**  
   Light butter or oil, toast cut-side down in a pan until golden. Set aside.

2. **Cook the veggies**  
   Hot skillet + oil → onions (and peppers). Cook until soft and slightly caramelized. Remove.

3. **Cook the steak**  
   Same pan, high heat.  
   Add Philly steak (break it up). Season lightly with salt, pepper, garlic powder.  
   Cook 4–6 minutes until browned. Splash of Worcestershire if you want.

4. **Melt the cheese**  
   Add veggies back in, lay cheese on top, cover pan 30–60 seconds until melted.

5. **Assemble**  
   Steak + cheese onto toasted roll. Add mayo if you like.

## 🥩 Steak Melt (Diner-Style)

### Ingredients
- Walmart Great Value Sirloin Philly Steak  
- **Bread:** Texas toast, thick white, or sourdough  
- **Cheese:** American (best melt) or provolone  
- Onion (optional but recommended)
- Butter or mayo
- Salt & black pepper  
- Optional: garlic powder, Worcestershire

---

### How to Make It

1. **Cook the steak**
   - Hot skillet, small oil.
   - Add Philly steak, break it up.
   - Season lightly (salt, pepper, garlic powder).
   - Cook 4–6 min until browned.
   - Splash Worcestershire if using. Remove.

2. **Cook onions (optional)**
   - Same pan, butter or oil.
   - Cook until soft and golden. Remove.

3. **Build the melt**
   - Butter or mayo **outside** of bread.
   - Inside layer order:
     - Bread  
     - Cheese  
     - Steak + onions  
     - Cheese  
     - Bread

4. **Grill it**
   - Medium-low heat.
   - Cook 3–4 min per side, press gently.
   - Flip once golden and crispy.
   - Cover pan last minute to fully melt cheese.

- Low heat = crispy bread, melted cheese
- Two slices of cheese = elite melt
- Let rest 1 min before cutting
