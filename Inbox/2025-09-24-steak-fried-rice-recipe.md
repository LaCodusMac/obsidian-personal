---

### Ingredients (2–3 servings)
- **8 oz steak** (sirloin, ribeye, or flank — thinly sliced against the grain)  
- **3 cups cooked rice** (preferably day-old, cold rice works best)  
- **2 eggs**  
- **1 cup mixed veggies** (peas, carrots, corn, or fresh diced veg)  
- **3 cloves garlic**, minced  
- **3–4 green onions**, chopped (white and green parts separated)  
- **3 tbsp soy sauce**  
- **1 tbsp oyster sauce** (optional, adds depth)  
- **1 tsp sesame oil**  
- **Cooking oil** (vegetable, peanut, or avocado oil for high heat)  
- **Salt & pepper**, to taste  

---

### Instructions
1. **Prep steak:**  
   - Slice steak thinly against the grain.  
   - Season with a little soy sauce, pepper, and a touch of cornstarch (optional, keeps it tender).  

2. **Heat wok:**  
   - Get your wok smoking hot. Add a drizzle of oil.  
   - Quickly sear the steak slices until browned but still a little pink (they’ll finish cooking later). Remove and set aside.  

3. **Scramble eggs:**  
   - Add a bit more oil. Crack in eggs, scramble, and push to the side or remove.  

4. **Aromatics & veggies:**  
   - Toss in garlic and the white parts of green onion, stir-fry 15–20 seconds.  
   - Add veggies and cook until just tender.  

5. **Rice:**  
   - Add cold rice, breaking up clumps. Stir-fry 2–3 minutes until hot and slightly crisped.  

6. **Sauce & steak back in:**  
   - Pour in soy sauce, oyster sauce (if using), and sesame oil. Mix well.  
   - Add steak and scrambled eggs back in. Toss until everything is coated and heated through.  

7. **Finish:**  
   - Sprinkle with the green tops of scallions. Taste and adjust with extra soy sauce, sesame oil, or pepper.

### What “toss quickly” means in wok cooking:  
When I say **toss quickly**, I mean moving the food around fast so it cooks evenly and doesn’t burn. In a wok:  
- You can use a spatula or wooden spoon to **scoop and flip** the rice/ingredients up and over.  
- Or, if you’re comfortable, you can use the wrist motion to **jerk the wok forward and up** so the food flips (like pros do).  

Basically, it’s constant motion over high heat — stir, scoop, flip, repeat — so nothing just sits there and scorches.

### Quick notes / reminders
- **What “toss quickly” looks like:** constant scooping/flipping with your spatula (or a quick wrist jerk with the wok if you’re confident). Keep food moving so it doesn’t burn and you get that light sear on rice.
- **Don’t overcrowd.** If your wok is small, cook in slightly smaller batches to keep the heat high.
- **If rice is clumpy/hard:** microwave 20–30 sec and then break it up before the wok.
- **Watch saltiness:** soy sauce adds salt fast — start with less and add at the end if needed.
