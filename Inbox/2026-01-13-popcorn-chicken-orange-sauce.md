### Easy Popcorn Chicken + Orange Sauce
**What you need**
- Frozen popcorn chicken (Tyson, Perdue, or store brand)
- Panda Express Orange Sauce (bottled)
- Optional: sesame seeds, green onions, red pepper flakes

**Steps**
1. **Cook the popcorn chicken**  
   - Air fryer: 400°F for 8–10 min (shake halfway)  
   - Oven: 425°F for ~15–18 min  
   You want it **extra crispy**.

2. **Warm the orange sauce**  
   - Heat gently in a small pan or microwave (don’t boil).

3. **Toss or drizzle**  
   - Toss chicken lightly in sauce for full coating  
   - OR drizzle to keep it crispier

4. **Finish**
   - Sprinkle sesame seeds + green onions  
   - Add red pepper flakes if you like heat 🌶️

### Make it a meal
- Serve over **white rice or fried rice**
- Add **steamed broccoli or snap peas**
- Throw it in a **wrap or rice bowl**
