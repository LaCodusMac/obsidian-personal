**ChatGPT:** Got it 👍 — you’re asking how to keep your **Microsoft Teams status light green (Available)** instead of going idle/yellow.  

Here’s what you should know:  

### Why it turns yellow  
- Teams automatically changes your status to **Away** after a few minutes of no activity (no mouse/keyboard input or Teams activity).  
- This is tied into your computer’s **presence** settings and Teams itself — there’s no official way to permanently lock it green.  

### What you *can* do  
1. **Manually set your status**  
   - Click your profile picture in Teams → Set status → choose **Available**.  
   - Add a **status duration** (e.g., "Reset status after: 8 hours") so it stays green until that time.  

2. **Keep your PC awake**  
   - If your computer goes to sleep or screen locks, Teams will turn you away.  
   - Change **power settings** so your display and PC don’t sleep while plugged in.  
   - On Windows: `Settings > System > Power & Battery`.  
   - On Mac: `System Settings > Displays > Advanced > Prevent automatic sleeping`.  

3. **Activity simulation** (gray area, but people do it)  
   - Some folks use “mouse jigglers” (small USB devices or apps) that keep the system active.  
   - This keeps Teams seeing you as active, so your light stays green.  

⚠️ Note: If this is for work, be mindful—IT admins might notice if you’re artificially keeping yourself “Available.”
