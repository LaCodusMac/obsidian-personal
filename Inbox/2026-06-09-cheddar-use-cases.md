### No grater?
You can:
- Slice thin strips with a knife.
- Use a vegetable peeler to make cheese ribbons.
- Cut tiny cubes for salads, eggs, or snacks.
- Crumble it with a knife if you just need it to melt.

### Pro tip
Put the cheese in the freezer for 10–15 minutes before grating. Extra sharp cheddar is often crumbly, and chilling it makes it much easier to shred cleanly.
