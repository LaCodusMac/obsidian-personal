**ChatGPT:** For a **thin ribeye steak** (say ½ inch to ¾ inch thick), the key is **quick, high-heat cooking** so it doesn’t overcook and dry out. Here are some good options:  

---

### **1. Pan-Searing**
- **Method:** Heat a cast-iron or heavy skillet over high heat, add a bit of oil with a high smoke point (canola, avocado, or grapeseed).  
- **Cooking:** 1–2 minutes per side for medium-rare, depending on thickness.  
- **Tips:**  
  - Season simply with salt and pepper.  
  - Optional: add a knob of butter and aromatics (garlic, thyme) at the end and baste the steak.  

---

### **2. Grilling**
- **Method:** Preheat grill to high.  
- **Cooking:** 1–2 minutes per side. Thin steaks cook so fast you need to watch carefully.  
- **Tip:** Direct heat is fine—no need for indirect unless you want a smoky char.  

---

### **3. Broiling (Oven)**
- **Method:** Place steak on a broiler pan ~4–6 inches from the heat source.  
- **Cooking:** 2–3 minutes per side, depending on thickness.  
- **Tip:** Flip halfway through and watch closely—thin cuts can go from perfect to overdone fast.  

---

### **4. Stir-Fry or Flash-Cook**
- **Method:** Slice thin ribeye into strips.  
- **Cooking:** Quickly cook in a hot wok or skillet for 30–60 seconds per side.  
- **Use:** Great for Asian-style dishes with sauces or veggies.  

---

### **Seasoning Ideas**
- **Classic:** Salt, pepper, garlic powder.  
- **Bold:** Paprika, cayenne, or Montreal steak spice.  
- **Finishing touch:** Butter, garlic, or fresh herbs after cooking.  

---

💡 **Pro Tip:** Because it’s thin, **let it come to room temperature** before cooking and **don’t overcook**—medium-rare is usually best.
