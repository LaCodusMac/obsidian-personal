**You:** “Bake the cookies, rotating halfway through.” Should I rotate cookies or pan? Do I rotate it 180 degrees?

**ChatGPT:** Rotate the **pan**, not the cookies 🙂

“Rotating halfway through” means:
- Take the **entire baking sheet** out
- Turn it **180 degrees**
- Put it back in the oven

This helps cookies bake evenly because most ovens have hot spots (the back or one side runs hotter).

**Do not**:
- Flip individual cookies
- Turn them over
- Move them around unless the recipe specifically says to

If you’re baking multiple trays on different racks, you’d usually:
- Swap **top ↔ bottom racks**
- Rotate each pan **180°** at the same time

You’re doing it right by asking—this is one of those recipe phrases that’s weirdly vague 🍪
