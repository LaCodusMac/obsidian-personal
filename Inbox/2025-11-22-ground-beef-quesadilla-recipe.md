## **🔥 Ground Beef Quesadilla (Simple & Fire)**

### **Ingredients (1–2 quesadillas)**
- ½ lb ground beef  
- 1 tsp salt  
- ½ tsp pepper  
- ½ tsp garlic powder  
- ½ tsp paprika or taco seasoning  
- Optional: diced onion or jalapeño  
- 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, Mexican blend, etc.)  
- 2 large flour tortillas  
- Optional: sour cream, salsa, hot sauce

---

## **🥘 Directions**

### **1. Cook the beef**
1. Heat a pan on medium-high.  
2. Add ground beef and break it up.  
3. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika/taco seasoning.  
4. Cook until browned (5–7 min).  
5. Drain excess fat if needed.

If you want it extra good:  
→ Add 1–2 tbsp salsa or a squirt of hot sauce to the beef after cooking to make it juicy.

---

### **2. Build the quesadilla**
1. Wipe the pan and lower heat to medium.  
2. Add a tortilla.  
3. Sprinkle a thin layer of cheese.  
4. Add a layer of the cooked beef.  
5. Add more cheese (helps glue it together).  
6. Top with the second tortilla.

---

### **3. Cook until crispy**
1. Cook 2–3 minutes per side until golden brown and cheese is melted.  
2. Press down with a spatula for a crisp finish.  
3. Slice into wedges.

---

## **🔥 Optional upgrades**
- Add sautéed onions/peppers  
- Mix beef with a little taco sauce  
- Add a thin spread of refried beans  
- Use pepper jack for heat  
- Add a scoop of cream cheese to the beef to make it creamy
