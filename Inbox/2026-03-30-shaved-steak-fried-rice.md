## What you need
- 1 lb shaved steak
- 4 cups **cold cooked rice** (day-old is best)
- 2 eggs
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 to 3 green onions, sliced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- Salt
- Black pepper

Optional but very good:
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire
- pinch of MSG
- drizzle of sriracha or yum yum on top

## Sauce mix
In a small bowl mix:
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire if using
- pinch of MSG if using

## How to make it
1. **Prep the steak**
   Season shaved steak lightly with salt and pepper. Don’t over-salt since sauce is salty.

2. **Cook the eggs**
   Heat a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add a little oil, scramble the eggs quickly, then remove.

3. **Cook the steak**
   Add 1 tbsp oil. Toss in shaved steak and let it get browned fast. Break it up as it cooks. Don’t stir too much at first. Once cooked, remove and set aside.

4. **Cook the veggies**
   Add remaining oil and butter. Cook onion 2 to 3 minutes. Add peas/carrots and garlic for about 1 minute.

5. **Fry the rice**
   Add the cold rice. Break up clumps and press it into the pan a bit so some gets lightly crisp. Let it sit between stirs.

6. **Add sauce**
   Pour sauce mix around the pan edges, not all in one spot. Toss rice well.

7. **Finish**
   Add steak and eggs back in. Toss everything together 1 to 2 minutes. Stir in green onion.

## Tips to make it taste like restaurant fried rice
- Use **cold rice**, never fresh hot rice
- Use **high heat**
- Don’t overcrowd the pan
- Let the rice sit a little so it fries, not steams
- Butter + soy + oyster sauce is the move
- A tiny pinch of MSG makes it taste much more takeout-style

## 🔥 Step-by-step (this is where most people mess up)

### 1. Break up your rice FIRST
Before cooking:
- Take rice out of fridge  
- Break up clumps with your hands  
- If it’s sticky → let it sit out 10 min  

👉 This is HUGE. Clumpy rice = mushy fried rice.

---

### 2. Cook egg (fast)
- Pan on **medium-high**
- Add a little oil  
- Crack egg, scramble quickly  
- Remove while still soft  

⏱ 30–45 seconds — don’t overcook

---

### 3. Cook shaved steak (important technique)
- Turn heat to **high**
- Add ~1 tsp oil  
- Lay steak down and **DON’T TOUCH for 30–60 sec**

👉 You want browning, not gray meat

- Then break it up, cook another minute  
- Remove once just cooked

---

### 4. Build flavor base
- Add another tsp oil + **butter**
- Add onion → cook 2–3 min until soft  
- Add peas & carrots → 1–2 min  
- Add garlic → 30 sec (don’t burn it)

---

### 5. Fry the rice (MOST IMPORTANT STEP)
- Add rice
- Immediately start breaking it apart in the pan
- Then:

👉 **Press it down into the pan**
👉 Let it sit 30–60 seconds between stirs

This is how you get that **slight crispy / toasted rice**

Do this for ~3–4 minutes total

---

### 6. Add sauce the right way
- Pour sauce **around the edges of the pan**, not directly on rice

👉 This lets it sizzle and caramelize slightly (restaurant trick)

- Toss everything quickly

---

### 7. Bring it all together
- Add steak + egg back in  
- Toss for 1–2 minutes  
- Add green onions at the end  

---

## 🔥 Pro tips (this is what makes it taste legit)

- **High heat = everything**
- Don’t crowd the pan (use biggest pan you have)
- Slightly under-sauce → you can always add more
- Butter + soy + oyster sauce = restaurant flavor combo
- MSG = optional but VERY noticeable difference

- Some pieces slightly crispy
