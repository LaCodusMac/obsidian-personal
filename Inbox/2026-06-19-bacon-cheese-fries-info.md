### Ingredients
- 1 lb frozen French fries (or potatoes cut into fries)
- 4–6 slices bacon
- 1–1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese
- Optional: green onions, sour cream, ranch, jalapeños

### Instructions

1. **Cook the bacon**
   - Oven: 400°F for 15–20 minutes on a sheet pan.
   - Let cool and chop into pieces.

2. **Cook the fries**
   - Bake according to package directions.
   - For extra crispiness, cook 5–10 minutes longer than the minimum time.

3. **Add toppings**
   - Spread fries on a sheet pan or oven-safe dish.
   - Sprinkle cheese evenly over the fries.
   - Add chopped bacon.

4. **Melt the cheese**
   - Return to the oven at 400°F for 3–5 minutes, or broil for 1–2 minutes until melted.

5. **Finish**
   - Add green onions, sour cream, ranch, or hot sauce if desired.

### If you're using frozen fries and thick-cut bacon
Cook the bacon first, then put the fries in the oven. When the fries have about 5 minutes left, add the cheese and bacon so everything finishes together.

### Simple Pan-Seared Petite Sirloin

**Ingredients**
- Petite sirloin steak
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Butter (optional)
- Garlic powder (optional)

### Instructions

1. Pat the steak dry.
2. Season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Let it sit at room temperature for 20–30 minutes if possible.
4. Heat a skillet over medium-high to high heat.
5. Add a small amount of oil if you have it (vegetable, canola, avocado, etc.).
6. Sear:
   - 1-inch steak: about 4–5 minutes per side for medium-rare.
   - Add a tablespoon of butter during the last minute if desired.
7. Rest for 5–10 minutes before slicing.

### Temperature Guide
- Rare: 125°F
- Medium-rare: 130–135°F
- Medium: 140–145°F
- Medium-well: 150°F+
