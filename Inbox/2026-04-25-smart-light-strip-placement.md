Where should I put smart light strips

**ChatGPT:** You’ve actually got a really clean galley kitchen—perfect for subtle lighting if you place it right. The key here is *indirect glow*, not visible strips.

### 🔥 Best placements (ranked)

**1. Under upper cabinets (top priority)**
- Run strips along the **bottom edge of both upper cabinet rows**
- This gives you:
  - Functional counter lighting (huge upgrade)
  - Clean, high-end look
- Hide the strip behind the front lip so you don’t see LEDs directly  
👉 This alone will transform the space

---

**2. On top of upper cabinets (ambient glow)**
- Place strips **on top of the cabinets facing upward**
- Creates a soft ceiling wash (great at night)
- Works especially well with your textured ceiling

👉 Use warm white here for a cozy vibe

---

**3. Behind the fridge edge (accent trick)**
- Run a short strip vertically **behind the fridge side facing the wall**
- Creates a subtle glow “cut” between fridge + wall

👉 Small detail, but looks super intentional

---

**4. Toe-kick lighting (optional but 🔥)**
- Along the bottom of lower cabinets facing the floor
- Gives a floating cabinet look at night

👉 Great for night lighting without blinding yourself

---

### ⚠️ Where NOT to put them
- ❌ Along exposed counter edges (you’ll see the strip = cheap look)
- ❌ Random wall runs (breaks the clean symmetry)
- ❌ Directly visible under microwave (unless diffused)

---

### 💡 Setup tips (important)
- Use **diffuser channels** if possible → removes LED “dots”
- Go **3000K warm white** (not blue/white)
- If RGB: keep color subtle (amber/soft white most of the time)
- Sync both sides so they feel balanced
