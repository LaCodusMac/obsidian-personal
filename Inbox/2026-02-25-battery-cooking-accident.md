## 🚨 If It Smoked, Leaked, or Burst

AA batteries (usually alkaline) contain **potassium hydroxide**, which is corrosive.

### Do this:

1. **Turn off heat immediately**
2. **Ventilate the room** (open windows, fan on)
3. Let everything cool completely
4. **Wear gloves** before touching anything
5. Throw away:
   - The battery
   - All food
   - Any absorbent materials (paper towels, etc.)

If you see **white powder or crust** → that’s leakage.

Clean surfaces with:
- Paper towels first
- Then wipe with **vinegar or lemon juice** (neutralizes alkaline residue)
- Then soap and water
