### Base Recipe (1 serving)  
**Ingredients:**  
- ½ cup rolled oats  
- ½ cup milk (any kind – dairy, almond, oat, etc.)  
- ¼ cup Greek yogurt (optional for creaminess + protein)  
- 1 tbsp chia seeds or ground flax (optional, for thickness & fiber)  
- 1–2 tsp honey, maple syrup, or sweetener of choice  
- ½ tsp vanilla extract (optional)  
- Pinch of cinnamon (optional)  

**Instructions:**

### Protein Overnight Oats (1 serving)  
**Ingredients:**  
- ½ cup rolled oats  
- ½ cup milk (dairy or high-protein milk like Fairlife/soy)  
- ¼ cup Greek yogurt (extra protein & creaminess)  
- 1 scoop protein powder (vanilla or chocolate works best)  
- 1 tbsp chia seeds or ground flax (optional, helps thicken & adds fiber)  
- 1–2 tsp honey or maple syrup (optional if protein powder is sweet)  
- Pinch of cinnamon or cocoa powder (optional)  

**Instructions:**  
1. Mix oats, milk, yogurt, protein powder, chia/flax, and sweetener in a jar or container.  
2. Stir well until smooth (protein powder should dissolve).  
3. Refrigerate overnight (or at least 4–6 hours).  
4. Stir before eating. If too thick, add a splash of milk.  

---
