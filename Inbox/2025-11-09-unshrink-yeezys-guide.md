**You:** My Yeezys shrunk in car heat. How to unshrink

**ChatGPT:** Ugh yeah, that happens. The heat basically tightened the knit and the foam midsole a bit. You *can* usually get them back close to normal size, but you gotta be gentle or you’ll warp them worse.

Here’s the best method people have had success with:

### What you’ll need
- Warm (not boiling) water
- A towel
- Shoe trees **or** thick socks stuffed inside the shoe
- Time + patience

---

### Step-by-step

1. **Stuff or stretch the shoe first**  
   Put **shoe trees** inside the Yeezys (ideal)  
   **If you don’t have shoe trees:**  
   - Put on **two thick socks** and slide your foot in.
   - The goal is to shape them back to your foot size before applying heat.

2. **Warm water wrap (NOT hot)**  
   - Wet a towel with **warm water** (think *hot shower temperature*, not boiling).
   - Wring it out so it’s damp, not dripping.
   - Wrap the shoe in the warm towel for **15–20 minutes**.

   This lets the materials loosen *slowly* instead of melting.

3. **Wear them while they cool**  
   - If you used your foot instead of a shoe tree, walk around in them for 20–30 minutes.
   - The shoe will mold back to your foot as it finishes cooling/drying.

4. **Let them air dry naturally**  
   - **NO** direct sunlight  
   - **NO** space heater  
   - **NO** hot dryer  
   Just room temp air.

---

### What **not** to do
- Don’t dip the shoes in hot water.
- Don’t use a hair dryer directly on them.
- Don’t put them back in the sun to “stretch” again.

Heat is what shrank them; we’re trying to gently *relax* the fibers, not nuke them.

**You:** Slides

**ChatGPT:** Got you. Yeezy **Slides** are easier to fix because they’re one big chunk of EVA foam. The heat basically tightened the foam cell structure. We just need to **gently re-expand** it without warping the shape.

### Here’s the safest way to “unshrink” Yeezy Slides:

#### You’ll need:
- A pot of **hot but not boiling** water
- A towel
- Thick socks (optional but recommended)
- Something to **stretch** them while cooling (your foot works best)

---

### Step-by-Step

1. **Heat water**  
   Bring water to just before a boil.  
   Think: steaming but not rolling bubbles.  
   If it’s boiling, **let it sit for 1–2 minutes**.

2. **Dip the slides (very controlled)**  
   - Hold the slides with tongs.
   - **Submerge them for 30–45 seconds**.  
   You’re warming the foam, not cooking it.

   They will feel slightly **softer** when you pull them out.

3. **Immediately put them on**  
   - Put on **thick socks** to avoid burning and to stretch them a bit more.
   - Slide your foot in while they’re warm.
   - Stand/walk around for **5–10 minutes** as they cool to room temp.

4. **Let them cool fully on your foot**  
   This step matters.  
   As they cool, the foam re-sets **to your foot size**.

---

### Important “Don’t Mess It Up” Notes:
- **Do NOT** leave them submerged too long. They will deform.
- **Do NOT** use a hair dryer. It heats unevenly and can bubble the surface.
- **Do NOT** stick them back in the sun. That’s how they shrank.

---

### If they still feel tight:
Repeat the warm-water dip for **another 20–30 seconds**.  
Don’t overdo multiple rounds in one session, let them fully cool first.
