**You:** How to make an omellete

**ChatGPT:** Here’s a simple way to make a classic omelet:  

### Ingredients  
- 2–3 eggs  
- 1–2 tbsp milk or water (optional, for fluffiness)  
- Salt & pepper, to taste  
- 1 tsp butter or oil  
- Fillings (optional): cheese, ham, mushrooms, onions, peppers, spinach, etc.  

### Steps  
1. **Crack and whisk**  
   - Crack the eggs into a bowl.  
   - Add a splash of milk or water (optional).  
   - Season with a little salt and pepper.  
   - Whisk with a fork until fully mixed and slightly frothy.  

2. **Heat the pan**  
   - Place a nonstick skillet over medium heat.  
   - Melt butter or heat oil until it lightly sizzles.  

3. **Cook the eggs**  
   - Pour the eggs into the pan and swirl to coat evenly.  
   - Let cook undisturbed for about 30–60 seconds until the edges start to set.  

4. **Add fillings** (if using)  
   - Sprinkle cheese, veggies, or meats on one side of the omelet.  

5. **Fold and finish**  
   - Use a spatula to gently fold the omelet in half over the fillings.  
   - Cook another 30–60 seconds until the inside is set but still soft.  

6. **Serve**  
   - Slide onto a plate and enjoy hot.
