## 🔥 What “thin” means

- Thin = about **½ inch (or less)**
- These are easy to overcook → timing matters more than anything

## 🍳 Best Method: Cast Iron (stove)
image_group{"aspect_ratio":"1:1","query":["cast iron steak sear butter garlic","thin steak cooking in pan high heat","steak flip tongs butter baste","steak crust close up cast iron"],"num_per_query":1}

### 1. Prep (important)
- Pat steak **dry**
- Salt + pepper both sides
- Let sit **10–15 min** (room temp helps even cook)

### 2. Get pan HOT
- Cast iron on high until lightly smoking  
- Add oil (not butter yet)

### 3. Cook FAST
- **45–60 sec per side**
- Flip once
- Press lightly for full contact

### 4. Butter baste (optional but 🔥)
- Add butter + garlic last 30 sec
- Spoon over steak

### 5. Rest
- 3–5 minutes (don’t skip)

---

## ⏱️ Doneness for thin steak
- Rare: ~45 sec/side  
- Medium rare: ~1 min/side (best target)  
- Medium: 1–1.5 min/side (easy to overdo)

👉 Anything past that = tough, dry

---

## ⚠️ Common mistakes
- ❌ Cooking too long (biggest one)
- ❌ Not enough heat → no crust
- ❌ Flipping constantly (just once is fine)
- ❌ Skipping rest → juices leak out

---

## 💡 Pro move (if it’s REALLY thin)
- Cook **30–45 sec per side**
- Slice immediately after rest → perfect for steak sandwiches or tacos
