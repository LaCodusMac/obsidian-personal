### Cheesy Ground Beef Quesadilla  
**Ingredients (2–3 quesadillas):**  
- 1 lb ground beef  
- 1 small onion, diced  
- 1 clove garlic, minced  
- 1 packet taco seasoning (or your own mix: chili powder, paprika, cumin, oregano, salt, pepper)  
- 1–2 tbsp tomato paste (optional, for richness)  
- 1/4 cup water  
- 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, Monterey jack, or Mexican blend)  
- 4–6 medium flour tortillas  
- Optional mix-ins: diced bell peppers, jalapeños, black beans, or corn  
- Oil or butter for cooking  

**Steps:**  
1. **Cook beef:** Heat skillet, add ground beef, onion, and garlic. Cook until beef is browned and onion is soft. Drain excess grease.  
2. **Season:** Add taco seasoning and tomato paste. Pour in water, stir, and simmer until thickened (2–3 mins).  
3. **Assemble quesadilla:** Heat another skillet or griddle on medium. Place tortilla down, sprinkle cheese, add a scoop of beef mixture, add more cheese, then top with another tortilla.  
4. **Cook:** Press gently and cook 2–3 minutes per side until golden brown and cheese is melted.  
5. **Serve:** Slice into wedges. Serve with sour cream, guacamole, and salsa.  

🔥 **Twist ideas:**  
- Add a layer of refried beans for extra creaminess.  
- Drizzle with hot sauce or chipotle mayo before folding.  
- Swap in pepper jack cheese for a spicy kick.

### Cooking Ground Beef
- **Medium-high heat** (around 6–7 out of 10 on most stoves) → hot enough to brown and get some flavor without steaming the meat.  
- Stir and break it up as it cooks, 6–8 minutes until no pink remains.  
- Once browned, turn heat to **medium** when adding seasoning + water so it simmers gently and thickens without burning.  

### Cooking the Quesadilla
- **Medium heat** is best.  
  - Too hot = tortilla burns before cheese melts.  
  - Too low = tortilla just dries out instead of crisping.  
- About **2–3 minutes per side**, press gently with a spatula until golden brown and cheese is melted.  

👉 A good test: the pan should lightly sizzle when the tortilla hits it, but not smoke.
