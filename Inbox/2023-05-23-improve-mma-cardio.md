4. Circuit training: Create circuit workouts that combine different exercises to target various muscle groups and improve cardiovascular endurance. Include exercises like kettlebell swings, medicine ball slams, battle ropes, and bodyweight movements. 5. Simulate fight conditions: Incorporate conditioning drills that mimic the pace and intensity of a fight. For example, you can set a timer for rounds lasting the duration of an MMA fight (typically 3-5 minutes) and perform continuous high-intensity exercises like shadow boxing, jumping jacks, or agility ladder drills during the round, followed by a short rest period. This helps improve your conditioning while simulating the specific demands of MMA.

6. Interval sparring: During training sessions, incorporate intervals of intense sparring followed by short rest periods. This not only enhances your cardio but also improves your technique and mental resilience under fatigue.
