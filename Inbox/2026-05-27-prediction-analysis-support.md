## Important Limitation

Markets are **probabilistic systems**, not deterministic systems.

No model can predict markets with certainty because price is influenced by:
- Liquidity
- News shocks
- Human behavior
- Central bank policy
- Geopolitics
- Positioning dynamics

So the focus should be:
- **Risk-adjusted probabilities**
- **Scenario planning**
- **Edge identification**
- **Capital preservation**
