**ChatGPT:** A New York strip makes a great rice bowl because it's flavorful enough that you don't need a complicated sauce.

### Steak & Rice Bowl (1–2 servings)

**Ingredients**
- 10–12 oz beef top loin (New York strip)
- 1½–2 cups cooked white rice
- 1 cup broccoli (or frozen mixed vegetables)
- ½ onion, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp butter
- Salt and black pepper
- Garlic powder
- Smoked paprika (optional)

**Sauce**
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (optional but recommended)
- 1 tsp honey or brown sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
- 1 tsp sriracha (optional)
- Splash of water

### Instructions

1. **Season the steak**
   - Pat dry.
   - Season generously with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and a little smoked paprika.
   - Let sit 20–30 minutes if possible.

2. **Cook the steak**
   - Heat a cast-iron or stainless skillet until very hot.
   - Add a little neutral oil.
   - Cook about **3–4 minutes per side** for medium-rare (adjust for thickness).
   - Add butter and garlic during the last minute and spoon over the steak.
   - Rest for 5–10 minutes before slicing.

3. **Cook the vegetables**
   - In the same pan, sauté onions for 3–4 minutes.
   - Add broccoli and cook until tender-crisp.
   - Pour in the sauce and cook for 1 minute.

4. **Build the bowl**
   - Rice on the bottom.
   - Vegetables.
   - Slice the steak thinly against the grain and place on top.
   - Spoon any remaining pan sauce over everything.

### Optional toppings
- Green onions
- Sesame seeds
- Sliced avocado
- Kimchi
- Fried or soft-boiled egg

If you're lifting, this bowl is excellent post-workout. A 10 oz strip with 2 cups of rice provides roughly:
- **Protein:** 60–70 g
- **Carbs:** 85–95 g
- **Fat:** 25–35 g (depending on how much fat is trimmed)
- **Calories:** ~850–1,050 kcal

It's filling, high in protein, and simple enough to make on a weeknight.

## Steak & Egg Rice Bowl (1 large serving)

### Ingredients
- 10–12 oz New York strip steak
- 2 cups cooked white rice
- 1 egg
- ½ onion, thinly sliced
- 1 cup broccoli (or other vegetables)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (avocado or vegetable)
- 1 tbsp butter

### Seasoning
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp garlic powder

### Sauce
Mix together in a small bowl:
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (or another tablespoon of soy sauce if you don't have it)
- 1 tsp honey or brown sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
- 1–2 tbsp water
- Sriracha to taste (optional)

---

## Step 1: Prep the steak
1. Remove the steak from the refrigerator 20–30 minutes before cooking.
2. Pat it very dry with paper towels.
3. Season both sides generously with the salt, pepper, and garlic powder.

---

## Step 2: Prep everything else
- Slice the onion.
- Mince the garlic.
- Warm the rice if it's leftover.
- Mix the sauce.

Have everything ready because the cooking goes quickly.

---

## Step 3: Cook the steak
1. Heat a cast-iron or stainless steel pan over **medium-high to high heat** for 2–3 minutes until very hot.
2. Add 1 tablespoon of oil.
3. Lay the steak in the pan away from you.

For a steak around **1 inch thick**:
- 3–4 minutes on the first side.
- Flip and cook another 3–4 minutes for medium-rare.

During the last minute:
- Add the butter and garlic.
- Tilt the pan and spoon the melted butter over the steak several times.

Remove the steak to a cutting board and let it **rest for 5–10 minutes**.

---

## Step 4: Cook the vegetables
Without cleaning the pan:
1. Add the onions.
2. Cook about 4 minutes until softened and lightly browned.
3. Add the broccoli and cook another 3–4 minutes.
4. Pour in the sauce and stir for about a minute until it lightly coats the vegetables.

---

## Step 5: Fry the egg
Use the same pan.

For a runny yolk:
1. Lower the heat to medium.
2. Add a tiny bit of oil or butter if needed.
3. Crack in the egg.
4. Cook about **2–3 minutes** until the white is set but the yolk is still runny.
5. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.

---

## Step 6: Slice the steak
Slice **against the grain** into thin strips. This keeps it tender.

---

## Step 7: Build the bowl
Layer in this order:
1. Rice
2. Vegetables with sauce
3. Steak slices
4. Fried egg on top

Finish with:
- Green onions (if you have them)
- Sesame seeds (optional)
- Extra soy sauce or sriracha to taste

When you eat it, break the egg yolk and mix everything together. The yolk combines with the pan sauce to make a rich coating for the steak and rice without needing any extra sauce.
