**You:** Found apartment key in driveway how to give back properly

**ChatGPT:** If you found an apartment key in a driveway, the safest and most proper way is to get it back to the property owner or management without putting yourself in an awkward or risky situation.

Good options, in order:

1. If you know which apartment/building it belongs to:
- Bring it to the leasing office or property manager.
- Say you found it in the driveway and wanted to turn it in.

2. If it has a building or unit tag:
- You can leave a short note on the building entrance or mailbox area like:
  “Found key in driveway — turned into management.”
- Avoid putting the exact unit number publicly if possible.

3. If there’s no office:
- Knock during daytime hours and ask if someone lost a key.
- Or leave it with a trusted nearby person like maintenance/security.

4. Avoid:
- Posting photos of the key online.
- Leaving it visible outside.
- Putting the full address/unit publicly on Facebook or Nextdoor.

If you’re unsure which apartment it belongs to, a good fallback is:
- Put it in a sealed envelope labeled “Found in driveway”
- Drop it in the apartment office mail slot or management mailbox.

That’s usually the cleanest and safest approach.
