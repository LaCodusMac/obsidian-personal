## 📈 What It Means

**Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)** = Investing a fixed amount of money at regular intervals (weekly, biweekly, monthly) regardless of market price.

Instead of trying to time the market:
- You buy more shares when prices are low  
- You buy fewer shares when prices are high  
- Over time, your average cost smooths out  

When people say “DCA the S&P,” they usually mean investing consistently into an index fund tracking the **entity["stock_market_index","S&P 500","us large cap index"]**.

## 💰 Example of DCA

Say you invest:

- $500/month  
- For 20 years  
- Average return ~8–10% annually  

You’re potentially looking at:
- ~$240,000 invested
- ~$500k–$600k total value (depending on returns)

The power comes from:
- Compounding  
- Staying invested  
- Ignoring short-term noise

## 🤔 Why DCA Instead of Lump Sum?

Statistically, lump sum investing wins more often.

BUT DCA wins psychologically:
- Reduces regret
- Removes timing pressure
- Encourages consistency
- Prevents sitting in cash too long

For most people, consistency > perfection.
