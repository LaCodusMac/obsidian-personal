### Step 1: **Determine Your Income**
- List all sources of income (e.g., salary, side hustles, passive income).

### Step 2: **Track Your Expenses**
- Identify and categorize your expenses into **fixed** and **variable**:
  - **Fixed expenses**: Rent, utilities, insurance, car payment, etc.
  - **Variable expenses**: Groceries, dining out, entertainment, shopping.

### Step 3: **Set Spending Percentages**
A common budgeting rule is the **50/30/20 rule**:
- **50% Needs**: Essentials like rent, groceries, utilities, transportation.
- **30% Wants**: Non-essentials like dining out, hobbies, subscriptions.
- **20% Savings/Debt**: Emergency fund, investments, or paying off debt.

#### Example (for $4,000/month income):
- **Needs (50%)**: $2,000
- **Wants (30%)**: $1,200
- **Savings/Debt (20%)**: $800
