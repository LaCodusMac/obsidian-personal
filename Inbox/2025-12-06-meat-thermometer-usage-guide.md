---

## ✅ **1. Know where to place it**
Placement is EVERYTHING.

### **Chicken (breast or thigh)**
- Insert into **the thickest part**  
- Avoid bones (bones give false high readings)

### **Steak / Beef / Pork**
- Insert from the **side**, not the top  
- Aim for the **center** of the thickest part  
- Don’t touch fat or bone

### **Ground beef patties / burgers**
- Insert **from the side** into the center

### **Roasts (beef, pork, whole chicken, turkey)**
- Insert into the **center of the thickest part**, usually:
  - Chicken/turkey: thigh (avoid the bone)
  - Beef/pork roasts: middle of the roast  

---

## ✅ **2. What temperature are you aiming for?**
Quick guide:

- **Chicken** → 165°F  
- **Ground beef** → 160°F  
- **Steak:**  
  - Rare: 125°F  
  - Medium rare: 135°F  
  - Medium: 145°F  
  - Medium well: 155°F  
- **Pork chops/loin** → 145°F (rest a few min)

---

## ✅ **3. When to check the temperature**
- Check near the **end of cooking**  
- If using a **digital instant-read**, insert and read in **2–3 seconds**  
- If using a **leave-in probe**, keep it in the meat while cooking (don’t use these on a pan-seared steak — better for oven/smoker)

---

## ✅ **4. Common mistakes**
❌ Touching bone → adds 10–15° falsely  
❌ Only checking one spot → always check the thickest area  
❌ Checking too early → pull food off heat too soon  
❌ Not letting steak rest → temp rises ~5° as it rests

## 🔥 **Pan-seared steak thermometer method**

### ✅ **1. Pull steak from pan briefly, insert thermometer, read, return (if needed)**  
- Lift the steak with tongs  
- Insert thermometer **from the side into the center**  
- Hold for **2–3 seconds**  
- If it needs more time, put it back in the pan

This prevents you from:
- Burning your hand  
- Melting your thermometer  
- Getting a false reading from touching the hot pan  

---

## ⏱ **When to check**
- Start checking ~20–30°F **before** your target temp because it cooks fast in a pan.

Example for **medium-rare (135°F)**:  
Start checking around **110–115°F**.

---

## 🌡 **Target pull temps (because it rises after you remove it)**

- Rare: pull at **120°F**  
- Medium-rare: pull at **130°F**  
- Medium: pull at **140°F**  
- Medium-well: pull at **150°F**  

Steak will rise ~5°F while resting.
