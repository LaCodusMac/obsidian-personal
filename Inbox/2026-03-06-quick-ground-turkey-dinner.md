## Ground Turkey Rice Bowl

image_group{"aspect_ratio":"1:1","query":["ground turkey rice bowl healthy dinner","ground turkey rice bowl with vegetables meal prep","simple ground turkey rice bowl skillet dinner","ground turkey soy garlic rice bowl"],"num_per_query":1}

### Ingredients (1–2 servings)
- 1 lb **ground turkey**
- 1 cup **cooked rice** (white or jasmine works great)
- 1 tbsp **olive oil**
- 1–2 cloves **garlic** (or garlic powder)
- 1/2 onion (optional but good)
- 1 cup **frozen veggies** (broccoli, peas, carrots, etc.)
- 2 tbsp **soy sauce**
- 1 tsp **sesame oil** (optional)
- Salt & pepper
- Optional: **sriracha or chili flakes**

---

### Instructions

1. **Heat pan**
   - Medium-high heat  
   - Add **olive oil**.

2. **Cook turkey**
   - Add ground turkey.  
   - Break it up with a spatula.  
   - Cook **6–7 minutes until browned**.

3. **Add flavor**
   - Add garlic + onion.  
   - Cook **1–2 minutes**.

4. **Add veggies**
   - Toss in frozen vegetables.  
   - Cook **3–4 minutes**.

5. **Season**
   - Add soy sauce, sesame oil, salt, pepper.  
   - Stir well.

6. **Serve**
   - Put mixture over rice.  
   - Add sriracha if you want heat.

---

### Approx Macros (per serving)
- **Calories:** ~450–500  
- **Protein:** ~35–40g  
- **Carbs:** ~40g  
- **Fat:** ~15g

## Cheesy Ground Turkey Skillet

image_group{"aspect_ratio":"1:1","query":["cheesy ground turkey skillet dinner","ground turkey cheese skillet comfort meal","ground turkey potato cheese skillet","hearty ground turkey skillet meal"],"num_per_query":1}

### Ingredients
- 1 lb **ground turkey**
- 1 **medium potato** (or 2 small), diced small  
- ½ **onion** (optional)
- 1 tbsp **olive oil**
- ½–1 cup **shredded cheese** (cheddar or Mexican blend)
- 1 tsp **garlic powder**
- 1 tsp **paprika**
- Salt & pepper
- Optional: **sour cream or hot sauce**

---

### Instructions

1. **Cook the potatoes first**
   - Heat **olive oil in a large pan** on medium.
   - Add diced potatoes with salt.
   - Cook **7–8 minutes**, stirring occasionally until soft and slightly crispy.

2. **Add turkey**
   - Push potatoes to the side.
   - Add ground turkey.
   - Break it up and cook **5–6 minutes**.

3. **Season**
   - Add onion, garlic powder, paprika, salt, pepper.
   - Mix everything together.

4. **Add cheese**
   - Sprinkle cheese on top.
   - Cover pan for **1–2 minutes** until melted.

5. **Serve**
   - Eat straight from the skillet.
   - Optional: add sour cream or hot sauce.

---

### Why it’s good for tonight
- **One pan**
- **Comfort food**
- **Very filling**
- Uses **basic ingredients**

## Ground Turkey Garlic Butter Noodles

This is **super filling and tastes way better than it should for how easy it is**.

### Ingredients
- 1 lb **ground turkey**
- ½ lb **pasta** (spaghetti, penne, whatever)
- 3 tbsp **butter**
- 3 cloves **garlic** (or garlic powder)
- **Parmesan cheese**
- Salt & pepper
- Optional: red pepper flakes

### Instructions

1. **Boil pasta**
   - Salted water.  
   - Cook until done (usually **8–10 min**).

2. **Cook turkey**
   - While pasta cooks, brown ground turkey in a pan on **medium-high**.  
   - Season with salt and pepper.

3. **Add butter + garlic**
   - Reduce heat to medium.  
   - Add butter and garlic.  
   - Let it melt and coat the turkey.

4. **Add noodles**
   - Drain pasta and toss it straight into the pan.

5. **Finish**
   - Add **parmesan**, mix well.  
   - Taste and add more butter/cheese if you want.

### Result
Rich, salty, buttery pasta with protein.  
**Perfect tired-night food.**

- **Shredded cheese**
- 1 tsp **chili powder or taco seasoning**
