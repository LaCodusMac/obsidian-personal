### 🌶️ Homemade Taco Seasoning
**Mix together:**
- 1 tbsp chili powder  
- 1 tsp ground cumin  
- 1 tsp paprika (smoked if you have it)  
- ½ tsp garlic powder  
- ½ tsp onion powder  
- ½ tsp dried oregano  
- ½ tsp salt  
- ¼–½ tsp black pepper  
- Optional: ¼ tsp cayenne (for heat)

### How to Use
- Use **2 tbsp seasoning per 1 lb of meat**
- Brown meat, drain fat
- Add seasoning + **⅔ cup water**
- Simmer 5 minutes until thick

### 🔥 Flavor Tweaks
- **Tex-Mex:** add ½ tsp sugar  
- **Restaurant-style:** add ¼ tsp MSG  
- **Smoky:** use smoked paprika + pinch of chipotle powder  
- **Low sodium:** reduce salt and boost cumin/garlic

### Bulk Version (Jar It)
Multiply everything by **4–6x** and store in an airtight jar for up to **6 months**.
