### 🔥 Simple Buffalo Sauce (No Worcestershire)
**Ingredients:**
- ½ cup **Frank’s RedHot** (or any cayenne hot sauce)  
- ¼ cup **unsalted butter**  
- 1 tbsp **white vinegar** *(optional but adds nice tang)*  
- ¼ tsp **garlic powder**  
- **Pinch of salt** (to taste)  

**Instructions:**
1. Melt the **butter** in a small saucepan over low heat.  
2. Whisk in the **hot sauce, vinegar, garlic powder**, and **salt**.  
3. Simmer gently for 2–3 minutes while stirring until smooth and slightly thickened.  
4. Let it cool slightly — it’ll thicken a bit more as it cools.  

You can toss wings in it right away or store it in the fridge up to a week.
