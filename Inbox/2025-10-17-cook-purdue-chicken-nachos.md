**ChatGPT:** Here’s a quick and tasty way to cook **Perdue diced chicken breast** for nachos:  

---

### 🔥 **Ingredients**
- 1 pack **Perdue diced chicken breast** (fresh or thawed)  
- 1–2 tsp **olive oil** or cooking spray  
- ½ tsp **salt**  
- ½ tsp **black pepper**  
- 1 tsp **chili powder**  
- ½ tsp **garlic powder**  
- ½ tsp **paprika**  
- Optional: ¼ tsp **cumin** or **smoked paprika** for extra flavor  

---

### 🍳 **Stovetop Method (Best Flavor)**
1. **Preheat pan:** Medium-high heat (about 7/10).  
2. **Add oil:** Once the pan is hot, coat with oil or spray.  
3. **Add chicken:** Spread evenly in the pan — don’t overcrowd.  
4. **Season:** Sprinkle with all your seasonings.  
5. **Cook:**  
   - Sauté for **5–7 minutes**, stirring occasionally.  
   - Chicken is done when it’s **golden brown** on the outside and reaches **165°F internal temp**.  
6. **Optional:** Add a splash of **lime juice** or **taco sauce** at the end for flavor.

---

### 🌮 **Nacho Assembly**
1. Spread **tortilla chips** on a baking sheet.  
2. Add cooked chicken evenly.  
3. Sprinkle on **cheese (cheddar, Monterey Jack, or a blend)**.  
4. Add extras: **black beans, jalapeños, corn, onions**, etc.  
5. **Bake at 375°F for 5–8 minutes**, until cheese is melted and bubbly.  
6. Top with **salsa, guac, sour cream, or cilantro** before serving.
