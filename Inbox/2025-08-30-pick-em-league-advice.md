**You:** Should I do a pick em league against the spread or no

**ChatGPT:** That depends on what you’re looking to get out of it:  

**Against the Spread (ATS):**  
- ✅ Makes it more challenging and skill-based (you’re not just picking the obvious winner).  
- ✅ Levels the playing field—upsets and blowouts matter less since spreads tighten things up.  
- ✅ Usually more fun for people who follow football closely.  
- ❌ Can be frustrating if you’re newer or just want casual fun (because even if your team wins, you might “lose” the pick).  

**Straight Up (no spread):**  
- ✅ Easier for casual fans, more about predicting winners.  
- ✅ Less stressful—especially if you’re playing with friends who don’t all live and breathe football.  
- ❌ Can get boring if one or two people run away with it by just picking favorites.  

👉 If your group is **mostly hardcore fans or gamblers at heart**, go **ATS**.  
👉 If it’s **more casual friends just looking for a fun pool**, go **straight up**.  

Do you want this league to be **competitive and skill-heavy**, or more **laid-back and social**?
