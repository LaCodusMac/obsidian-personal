## 🍊 Best Liquor to Mix with **Orange Juice (OJ)**

These pairings make tasty, easy-to-make highball-style drinks or simple brunch classics:

### 🥃 1. **Vodka**
- **Classic & clean** — lets the orange shine.
- Works great with ice and a splash of OJ.
- Optional: Add a maraschino cherry or orange wheel.

**Ratio (starter)**:  
🥃 1.5 oz vodka + 4–6 oz OJ

---

### 🍹 2. **Rum (Light/Gold)**
- **Smoother & slightly sweet**, complements the citrus.
- Light rum keeps it crisp; gold adds a hint of caramel.

**Ratio**:  
🥃 1.5 oz rum + 4–6 oz OJ

---

### 🍊 3. **Tequila (Blanco or Reposado)**
- Gives more **character and zest** than vodka or rum.
- Great brunch vibe.

**Ratio**:  
🥃 1.5 oz tequila + 4–6 oz OJ

---

### 🥂 4. **Champagne / Sparkling Wine (Not liquor, but popular)**

🥂 Equal parts sparkling + OJ

## 🥤 Best Liquor to Mix with **Diet Coke**

These pairings balance sweetness and carbonation nicely:

### 🥃 1. **Bourbon**
- **Rich and mellow** with vanilla/caramel notes — great with the cola bite.

**Ratio**:  
🥃 1.5 oz bourbon + diet coke to top

---

### 🥃 2. **Whiskey (Rye or Irish)**
- Rye adds spicy complexity; Irish is smooth and light.

**Ratio**:  
🥃 1.5 oz whiskey + diet coke

---

### 🥃 3. **Rum (Dark)**
- Dark rum brings **molasses & spice** that echo the cola base.

**Ratio**:  
🥃 1.5 oz dark rum + diet coke

---

### 🥃 4. **Spiced Rum**
- Sweet spices pair exceptionally well with coke’s flavor.

**Ratio**:  
🥃 1.5 oz spiced rum + diet coke

## 🍸 Tips for Great Mixed Drinks
- **Ice matters:** Fill the glass ¾ with ice for a cold, balanced drink.  
- **Glassware:**  
  - **Tall glass** for OJ drinks  
  - **Highball** for diet coke mixes  
- **Fresh garnish:** Orange slices or lime wedges add aroma & style.  
- **Start simple:** Adjust ratios to taste — more mixer if you want it lighter.
