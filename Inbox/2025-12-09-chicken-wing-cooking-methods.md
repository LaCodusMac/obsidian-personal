# 🔥 **1. Oven-Baked Crispy Wings (Best all-around)**
**Texture:** Crispy skin, juicy inside  
**Time:** ~50 minutes

### **Ingredients**
- 2–3 lbs chicken wings  
- 1 tbsp baking powder (**not** baking soda)  
- 1 tsp salt  
- 1 tsp black pepper  
- 1–2 tsp garlic powder  
- Optional: paprika, cayenne  

### **Instructions**
1. **Pat wings dry** with paper towels.  
2. Toss in **baking powder + seasonings**. (This makes the skin crisp.)  
3. Place wings **on a wire rack** over a baking sheet (helps crispiness).  
4. Bake at **425°F (218°C)** for **45–50 minutes**, flipping halfway.  
5. Toss in sauce or leave dry.

---

# ⚡ **2. Air Fryer Wings (Fast & very crispy)**
**Texture:** Crispy like deep-fried  
**Time:** 20–25 minutes

### Steps
1. Dry wings.  
2. Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, maybe a little baking powder.  
3. Air fry at **400°F** for **20–25 min**, shake halfway.  
4. Sauce after cooking.

---

# 🍳 **3. Deep-Fried Wings (Best taste)**
**Texture:** Restaurant-level crisp  
**Time:** 10–12 minutes

### Steps
1. Heat oil to **350°F**.  
2. Pat wings dry (no flour needed, but you can toss in cornstarch for extra crisp).  
3. Fry **10–12 min** until golden.  
4. Toss in sauce.

# 🔥 Great Seasoning / Sauce Ideas
### **Dry Rub**
- Salt  
- Pepper  
- Garlic powder  
- Paprika  
- Cayenne  
- Brown sugar (optional)

### **Buffalo**
- 1/2 cup Frank’s RedHot  
- 4 tbsp melted butter  
- 1 tsp garlic powder  
- Mix and toss wings immediately after cooking.

### **Lemon Pepper**
- Toss cooked wings in:  
  - 2 tbsp melted butter  
  - 1–2 tsp lemon pepper seasoning

### **Honey Garlic**
- 1/4 cup honey  
- 2 tbsp soy sauce  
- 3 cloves garlic (minced)  
- Simmer until slightly thick, then coat wings.

# 🔥 **1. Basic Juicy Ground Turkey (for anything)**
Works for rice bowls, meal prep, pasta, tacos, etc.

### **Ingredients**
- 1 lb ground turkey (93/7 or 90/10 tastes best)  
- 1 tbsp olive oil or butter  
- Salt + pepper  
- Garlic powder  
- Onion powder  
- Optional: paprika

### **Instructions**
1. **Heat pan** on medium-high with a little oil.  
2. Add turkey and **don’t stir for 2–3 minutes**—let it brown.  
3. Break it up, season with salt/pepper/garlic/onion.  
4. Cook **6–8 minutes** until no pink, and lightly browned.  
5. Taste → adjust salt.  

**Tip:**  
Turkey dries out fast. If it seems dry, add **2–3 tbsp chicken broth** at the end and mix in.

# 🌮 **2. Turkey Taco Meat (super good)**
### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground turkey  
- 1 packet taco seasoning (or homemade)  
- 1/3 cup water  
- Optional: small diced onion

### Instructions
1. Brown turkey.  
2. Add seasoning + water.  
3. Simmer **3–4 minutes** until thick.  

Serve with tortillas, rice bowls, nachos, etc.

# 🍚 **3. Teriyaki Turkey (fast Asian-style)**
Amazing for rice bowls.

### Ingredients
- 1 lb turkey  
- 2 tbsp soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp brown sugar or honey  
- 1 tsp garlic  
- 1 tsp ginger  
- Optional: splash of sriracha

### Instructions
1. Brown turkey.  
2. Stir in soy sauce + sugar + garlic + ginger.  
3. Cook until glossy and slightly sticky.

Serve over rice with green onions.

# 🥡 **4. Turkey Fried Rice**
### Ingredients
- 1 lb turkey  
- 2 cups cooked rice  
- Frozen veggies  
- 2 eggs  
- Soy sauce + garlic

### Instructions
1. Brown turkey. Remove.  
2. Cook veggies. Add rice.  
3. Push aside, scramble eggs.  
4. Add turkey back → season with soy sauce + garlic.

# 🥘 **5. Turkey & Rice Meal Prep (clean & filling)**
### Ingredients
- 1 lb ground turkey  
- 1 cup rice  
- Salt, pepper, garlic, paprika  
- Optional: broccoli

### Instructions
1. Cook rice.  
2. Brown turkey with seasonings.  
3. Add a splash of broth to keep juicy.  
4. Portion with rice + veggies.
