## Today's Leg Workout

### Warm-Up
- 5-10 min incline walk or bike
- Bodyweight squats × 10
- Walking lunges × 10/leg

- Leg swings

### Main Lift: Back Squat
Work up gradually:

- Empty bar × 10
- 95 × 5
- 115 × 5
- 135 × 5
- 155 × 5
- 175 × 5

Then:

**3 sets of 5 at a weight that feels like you could do 2-3 more reps**

For most former 225+ bench / 340 squat lifters coming back after a long layoff, this will probably be somewhere around **155-185 lbs**, but go by feel.

### Romanian Deadlift

### Leg Press

### Walking Lunges

### Calf Raises

### Optional Finisher

- 10 minutes incline treadmill

## What NOT To Do
- Don't test your max.
- Don't try to squat 300 because you used to.
- Don't do 20 sets of accessories because you're motivated.

Your goal is to leave the gym feeling like:
> "I definitely could have done more."

That's exactly where you want to be before starting 5/3/1.

## Starting 5/3/1 Next Week
Given your history (former 340 squat, 215 bench, half-marathon fitness), I'd spend **2-3 weeks rebuilding** before calculating true training maxes.

- Bench TM: 165-175
- Squat TM: 225-245
- Deadlift TM: 245-275

Even if those feel light, starting too light is one of the reasons 5/3/1 works.
