**You:** How long after cooking rice is cold rice good for

**ChatGPT:** Cold rice is one of those things where timing actually matters a lot more than people think.

### ⏱️ Safe timeline
- **Up to ~1 hour after cooking** → fine at room temp  
- **1–2 hours** → still okay, but should be cooled ASAP  
- **After 2 hours at room temp** → ❌ *don’t eat it*  

### 🧊 Once refrigerated properly
- Put rice in the fridge **within 1–2 hours of cooking**
- Then it’s good for:
  - **3–4 days in the fridge**
- Reheat until it’s **steaming hot all the way through**

---

### ⚠️ Why rice is different
Rice can carry bacteria called *Bacillus cereus*. Cooking doesn’t always kill its spores, and if rice sits out:
- bacteria multiply  
- toxins form  
- reheating **won’t fix it**

---

### 🍚 For fried rice (what you were asking earlier)
Best setup:
- Cool rice quickly (spread it out if possible)
- Refrigerate overnight  
- Use within **1–2 days for best texture**

---

### 💡 Quick rule of thumb
If rice sat out overnight on “warm” or the counter → **not worth the risk**, even if it smells fine.
