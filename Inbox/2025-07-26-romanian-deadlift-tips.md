**ChatGPT:** Here are some essential **tips for Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs)** to help you maximize results and avoid injury:

---

### 🧠 **Form & Setup Tips**
1. **Feet Position**: Stand with feet hip-width apart, toes forward or slightly turned out.
2. **Grip**: Hold the barbell or dumbbells in front of you with an overhand grip, hands just outside your thighs.
3. **Soft Knees**: Keep a **slight bend** in your knees—don't lock them out.
4. **Flat Back**: Maintain a **neutral spine** (no rounding or hyperextension). Think “chest proud, shoulder blades back.”
5. **Hinge at the hips**: Push your hips **back**, not down. It’s not a squat.

---

### 🔁 **Execution Tips**
6. **Bar/Dumbbell Path**: Keep the bar/dumbbells **close to your legs** the whole time—graze your thighs/shins.
7. **Depth**: Lower until you feel a good **stretch in your hamstrings**, usually when the weights reach around mid-shin. Don’t force range with poor form.
8. **Neck Alignment**: Keep your neck neutral—look a few feet in front of you on the floor, not up or down.
9. **Drive Through Heels**: On the way up, drive through your **heels** and engage your **glutes and hamstrings** to return to standing.
10. **Control the Tempo**: Lower slowly (2–3 seconds), pause at the bottom briefly, and come up strong but controlled.

---

### ✅ **Extra Tips**
- **Warm Up**: Loosen up your hamstrings and glutes first—leg swings, glute bridges, etc.
- **Use Chalk or Straps**: If grip limits you, chalk or lifting straps can help.
- **Don’t Go Too Heavy**: This is a tension-focused movement, not a max lift—**form > ego**.
- **Breathe Properly**: Inhale as you lower, brace your core, and exhale as you return to standing.
