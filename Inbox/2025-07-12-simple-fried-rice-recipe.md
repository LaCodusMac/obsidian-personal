### 🍳 **Simple Fried Rice Recipe**

**Ingredients (Serves 2–3):**
- 2 cups **cold cooked rice** (day-old rice works best)
- 2 tbsp **oil** (vegetable, canola, or sesame)
- 2 **eggs** (beaten)
- 1/2 cup **diced onion**
- 2 cloves **garlic**, minced
- 1/2 cup **frozen peas and carrots** (or any veggies)
- 2–3 tbsp **soy sauce**
- 1 tbsp **oyster sauce** (optional for richness)
- 1 tsp **sesame oil** (optional for flavor)
- **Green onions** or scallions (chopped, optional)
- Cooked protein: **chicken, shrimp, pork, or tofu** (optional)

---

### 🔪 Instructions:

1. **Prep**: Break up cold rice with your hands or a spoon so it’s loose.
2. **Scramble Eggs**: Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Pour in beaten eggs and scramble. Remove once cooked.
3. **Stir-Fry Veggies**: Add another tbsp oil, sauté onions and garlic until fragrant (about 1 min), then add frozen veggies. Cook until heated through.
4. **Add Rice**: Toss in rice. Stir-fry, pressing and flipping to mix well and heat through.
5. **Season**: Add soy sauce, oyster sauce (if using), and sesame oil. Stir to evenly coat everything.
6. **Finish**: Return scrambled eggs and cooked protein to the pan. Mix thoroughly.
7. **Top & Serve**: Garnish with green onions if desired.

---

### 🔁 Variations:
- Add **chili paste** or **sriracha** for heat.
- Try **kimchi** or **pineapple** for a twist.
- Use **leftover rotisserie chicken** or **bacon** for extra flavor.

### 🔥 **Quick Pan-Seared Thin Sirloin Steak**

**What You Need:**
- Thin-cut **sirloin steak** (about 1/4" thick)
- Salt & pepper (or your favorite steak seasoning)
- 1–2 tsp **oil** (vegetable, canola, or avocado oil)
- Optional: garlic powder, paprika, soy sauce, or Worcestershire sauce

---

### 🕒 **Quick Instructions:**

1. **Bring to Room Temp**  
   Take steak out of the fridge 15–20 min before cooking.

2. **Season**  
   Pat dry with paper towels. Season generously with salt, pepper, and any other spices.

3. **Heat Pan**  
   Heat oil in a cast iron or stainless steel pan over **medium-high** until shimmering.

4. **Sear**  
   Add steak to pan. Cook **about 1 minute per side** (or until browned). Don’t crowd the pan.

5. **Optional – Baste for Flavor**  
   Add a knob of butter, crushed garlic, and fresh herbs (like thyme) in the last 30 seconds, and spoon over the steak.

6. **Rest**  
   Let steak rest for **5 minutes** on a plate before slicing. Slice **against the grain** for tenderness.

## 🥢 Steak Fried Rice

**Ingredients (2–3 servings)**  
- 2 cups **cold cooked rice** (day-old is best)  
- 6–8 oz **thin-cut sirloin**, sliced into small bite-size strips  
- 2 tbsp **oil** (vegetable, canola, or sesame)  
- 2 **eggs**, beaten  
- 1/2 cup **onion**, diced  
- 1 cup **frozen peas and carrots** (or mixed vegetables)  
- 2–3 tbsp **soy sauce**  
- 1 tbsp **oyster sauce** (optional, for depth)  
- 1 tsp **sesame oil** (optional, for aroma)  
- **Salt & pepper** to taste  
- **Green onions**, chopped (optional garnish)  

---

**Instructions**  

1. **Prep Steak** – Pat dry, season lightly with salt & pepper.  
2. **Cook Steak** – Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan or wok over medium-high. Sear steak 1–2 min per side until browned. Remove and set aside.  
3. **Scramble Eggs** – Add a little oil if needed, pour in beaten eggs, scramble until just cooked. Remove and set aside with steak.  
4. **Cook Veggies** – Add 1 tbsp oil, sauté onion 1–2 min. Add peas and carrots, stir until heated through.  
5. **Add Rice** – Break up cold rice, add to pan. Stir-fry 2–3 min until hot.  
6. **Season** – Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, sesame oil. Stir well.  
7. **Combine** – Return steak and eggs. Toss until evenly mixed and hot.  
8. **Finish** – Taste and adjust seasoning. Garnish with green onions if desired.
