**ChatGPT:** 🔥 **Shaved Steak Grilled Cheese (Philly-Style)** 🔥

**Ingredients**
- Shaved steak (ribeye if possible)
- Sliced bread (sourdough or white)
- Cheese: provolone, American, or mozzarella (or a combo)
- Butter or mayo (for the bread)
- Optional add-ins: sautéed onions, peppers, mushrooms
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder

**Steps**
1. **Cook the steak**
   - Heat a skillet on medium-high with a little oil.
   - Add shaved steak, season lightly with salt, pepper, garlic powder.
   - Cook fast (2–3 min), chopping it up as it browns. Remove from pan.

2. **Optional veggies**
   - In the same pan, sauté onions/peppers until soft. Mix with steak.

3. **Assemble**
   - Butter (or mayo) the outside of the bread.
   - Inside: layer cheese → steak → more cheese (cheese on both sides helps glue it).

4. **Grill**
   - Cook on medium-low so the cheese melts without burning.
   - Flip once, press lightly, cook until golden and melty.

**Pro Tips**
- Mayo on the outside = extra crispy, restaurant-style crust
- Add a splash of Worcestershire or steak sauce to the meat for extra flavor
- Wrap in foil for 1–2 minutes after cooking for peak melt 🧀
