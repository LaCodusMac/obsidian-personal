**You:** P0300 code for car

**ChatGPT:** A **P0300** code means your car has a **random/multiple cylinder misfire**. That’s different from codes like P0301–P0308 (which point to a specific cylinder). P0300 basically says: *“engine is misfiring, but not consistently in one spot.”*

---

## 🚗 What it feels like
- Rough idle (car shaking at stops)
- Loss of power / sluggish acceleration
- Engine hesitation or jerking
- Check engine light (sometimes flashing = urgent)

---

## 🔧 Most common causes (in order of likelihood)

### 1. Ignition issues (most common)
- Worn **spark plugs**
- Bad **ignition coils**
- Faulty plug wires (older cars)

👉 If you haven’t done plugs in ~60–100k miles, start here.

---

### 2. Fuel system problems
- Dirty or failing **fuel injectors**
- Weak **fuel pump**
- Clogged **fuel filter**

---

### 3. Air / vacuum issues
- **Vacuum leak** (cracked hose, intake leak)
- Dirty or failing **mass airflow sensor (MAF)**
- Throttle body buildup

---

### 4. Engine / mechanical issues (less common but serious)
- Low compression (worn piston rings, valves)
- Timing issues

---

### 5. Sensor-related
- Bad **oxygen (O2) sensor**
- Crankshaft or camshaft position sensor

---

## ⚠️ How urgent is it?
- **Flashing check engine light** → Stop driving ASAP (can damage catalytic converter)
- **Solid light** → You can drive short-term, but fix soon

---

## 🧠 Quick troubleshooting (best bang for effort)

Start simple before throwing parts at it:

1. **Scan for other codes**
   - If you also see P0301–P030X → helps pinpoint cylinder

2. **Check spark plugs**
   - Cheap and often the fix

3. **Swap ignition coils**
   - Move coil from one cylinder to another
   - See if misfire follows → bad coil

4. **Check for vacuum leaks**
   - Listen for hissing
   - Spray carb cleaner around intake (if idle changes → leak)

5. **Clean MAF sensor**
   - Very common fix

---

- Spark plugs: $40–$150

- MAF cleaning: $10 (DIY)
- Injectors/pump: $200–$1000+
- Engine issues: $$$
