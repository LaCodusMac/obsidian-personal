# 🔥 **Beef & Rice Meal Prep (Simple, Flavorful, High Protein)**

### **Ingredients (4–5 meals)**
- **1.5 lb ground beef** (90/10 or 93/7)
- **2 cups uncooked rice** (white or jasmine)
- **1 medium onion**, diced (optional but adds flavor)
- **3–4 cloves garlic**, minced OR 1 tsp garlic powder  
- **1 tbsp soy sauce**  
- **1 tbsp oyster sauce** (optional but worth it)
- **1 tbsp chili garlic sauce** OR sriracha  
- **1–2 tsp paprika**  
- **Salt & pepper**
- **Frozen veggies** (broccoli, mixed veggies, corn — up to 3 cups)
- **1–2 tbsp olive oil**

---

# 🍳 **How to Make It**

### **1. Cook the rice**
- Rinse rice  
- Add 2 cups rice + 3 cups water to rice cooker or pot  
- Let it cook while you make the beef

---

### **2. Brown the beef**
- Heat pan on **medium-high**  
- Add oil → add ground beef  
- Season with salt, pepper, paprika  
- Break it apart and cook until mostly browned  

---

### **3. Add aromatics & veggies**
- Add onion → cook 3–4 minutes  
- Add garlic → cook 1 minute  
- Add frozen veggies → toss in and cook until heated through  

---

### **4. Flavor it**
Stir in:
- Soy sauce  
- Oyster sauce  
- Chili garlic or sriracha  
Taste → add more soy or spice if needed.

(If you want it sweeter, add **1–2 tsp brown sugar**.)

---

### **5. Plate for meal prep**
Add rice to containers  
Top with beef & veggies  
Finish with:
- Green onions  
- Extra sriracha  
- Sesame seeds (optional)

---

# 💪 **Macros (approx per serving, 5 servings)**
- **~450–550 calories**
