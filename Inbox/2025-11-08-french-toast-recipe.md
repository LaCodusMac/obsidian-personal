**You:** French toast redipe

**ChatGPT:** Got you. Here’s a super reliable, tasty French toast recipe that comes out golden and custardy, not soggy.

**What you need:**  
- 2 eggs  
- 1/3 cup milk (whole is best, but whatever you got works)  
- 1–2 teaspoons sugar (optional but good)  
- A splash of **vanilla**  
- A pinch of **cinnamon** (don’t go crazy, just a sprinkle)  
- Bread: Brioche or Texas toast is elite, but any sandwich bread works  
- Butter for the pan  

**How to do it:**  
1. In a wide bowl, whisk **eggs + milk + sugar + vanilla + cinnamon** until smooth.  
2. Heat a pan to **medium** and melt some butter in it.  
3. Dip each slice of bread in the mixture for a couple seconds per side. Don’t fully drown it.  
4. Set it on the pan and cook until **golden brown**, around 2–3 minutes per side.  
5. Serve with syrup, powdered sugar, berries, whatever you vibe with.

**Quick tips:**  
- If your bread is a little stale or toasted lightly first, it soaks perfectly and doesn’t get mushy.  
- Don’t crank the heat high; you’ll burn the outside and have raw egg in the middle. Medium is the move.
