**You:** PnL

**ChatGPT:** PnL = **Profit and Loss**.

In trading (like your Kalshi/Hermes setup), it’s basically:

> **How much money you made or lost**

Examples:

- Bought contracts for $40 total
- Sold them for $55 total
- Your PnL = **+$15**

Or:

- Bought for $80
- Market moved against you
- Position now worth $50
- Your PnL = **-$30**

There are usually two types:

- **Realized PnL**
  - Actual locked-in profit/loss after closing a trade

- **Unrealized PnL**
  - “Paper” gains/losses while still holding the position

For your Hermes demo trader, tracking PnL becomes important because it tells you:

- whether signals are actually profitable
- which market groups work best
- if spread/slippage is killing returns
- whether risk rules are helping or hurting

- spreads are too wide
- liquidity is thin

- exits are hard

So your bot eventually needs:
- signal quality
- execution quality
- PnL tracking
