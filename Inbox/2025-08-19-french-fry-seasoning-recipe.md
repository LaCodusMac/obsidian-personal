**Basic French Fry Seasoning** (makes about ½ cup)  
- 2 tbsp salt (or seasoned salt)  
- 1 tbsp paprika (smoked if you like extra flavor)  
- 1 tbsp garlic powder  
- 1 tbsp onion powder  
- 1 tsp black pepper  
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)  
- 1 tsp dried parsley (optional, for freshness)  

**Directions:**  
1. Mix everything together in a small bowl or jar.  
2. Fry or bake your fries, then sprinkle the seasoning right after they come out hot (so it sticks).  
3. Toss to coat evenly.  

👉 You can also swap paprika for chili powder, or add parmesan for a cheesy version.
