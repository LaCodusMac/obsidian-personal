**You:** Does a vacuum kill spiders quick

**ChatGPT:** A vacuum **can kill spiders**, but whether it kills them **quickly** depends on a few factors:

### ✅ What usually happens:
- **Small spiders**: Typically **die quickly** inside the vacuum due to the trauma of being sucked up, the impact, or suffocation (especially in bagged vacuums).
- **Larger spiders**: May **survive the initial suction**, especially if your vacuum is bagless and doesn’t have strong filters or brushes that damage them.

### 🕷️ What can increase the chance of killing them:
- **Use a vacuum with a bag**: Spiders are more likely to suffocate or be crushed inside.
- **Keep the vacuum running for a few seconds** after sucking up the spider to ensure it travels deep inside.
- **Empty or discard the bag/bin afterward**, or spray inside it with insecticide if you're concerned the spider survived.

### Bottom line:
A vacuum will usually **kill spiders quickly**, especially smaller ones. But to be safe, **assume survival is possible** unless you empty or dispose of the contents.
