A few additions I'd make specifically for you:

- Ask me to compare new photos against old photos when possible.
- Factor in my lifting goals, running/cardio goals, and athletic performance, not just aesthetics.
- Tell me when a recommendation is not worth the effort relative to its visual impact.
- Separate "looksmax" advice into:
  - Tier 1: Major impact
  - Tier 2: Moderate impact
  - Tier 3: Minor/detail improvements

That tends to prevent getting stuck obsessing over tiny details while ignoring the bigger drivers of appearance: body composition, muscle mass, grooming consistency, posture, sleep, and confidence.
