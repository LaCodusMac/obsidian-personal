**You:** Shaved steak dinner ideas

**ChatGPT:** Shaved steak is clutch because it cooks fast and soaks up flavor. Here are some **easy but solid dinner ideas** (most are 15–25 min meals):

---

## 🧀 1. Philly Cheesesteak (classic)
**How:**  
Sauté onions + peppers in butter/oil → add shaved steak → salt, pepper, garlic → melt provolone or American on top.  
Serve on hoagie or over fries.

**Upgrade:** Add mushrooms + a little Worcestershire.

---

## 🌮 2. Steak Tacos / Quesadillas
Season steak with:
- Chili powder  
- Cumin  
- Garlic  
- Lime

Quick sear → tortillas with cheese, salsa, sour cream.

---

## 🍜 3. Steak Stir Fry
Throw it in a hot pan with:
- Frozen stir-fry veggies  
- Soy sauce + garlic + ginger  
- Finish with sesame oil

Serve over rice or noodles.

---

## 🥩 4. Steak & Eggs (dinner version)
Shaved steak with:
- Onions  
- Over-easy eggs  
- Hash browns or toast

Elite lazy dinner.

---

## 🍝 5. Steak Alfredo or Garlic Butter Pasta
Sear steak → set aside →  
Cream + garlic + parm → toss with pasta → add steak back.

---

## 🧂 6. Steak Rice Bowl (meal prep style)
Base:
- Rice  
- Steak  
- Veg (broccoli, peppers, onions)

Sauce ideas:
- Teriyaki  
- Chipotle mayo  
- Garlic butter

---

## 🥪 7. Steak Grilled Cheese
Steak + onions + provolone inside buttery sourdough.

Basically a **Philly melt**.

---

## 🌶️ 8. Mongolian Beef (fake takeout)
Sauce:
- Soy  
- Brown sugar  
- Garlic  
- Ginger  
- Splash of water + cornstarch

Serve with rice and green onions.

---

## 🥔 9. Steak & Loaded Potatoes
Bake potatoes → top with:
- Steak  
- Cheese  
- Sour cream  
- Chives

Steakhouse vibes.

---

## 🧄 10. Garlic Butter Steak Skillet
Just steak, butter, garlic, salt, pepper.  
Serve with:
- Green beans  
- Asparagus  
- Or straight over rice.

---

### Pro Tip for Shaved Steak
Because it’s thin:
- **High heat**
- **Don’t overcook** (2–3 min)
- Season at the end or it dries out.
