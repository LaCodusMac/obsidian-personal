**ChatGPT:** From your screenshot, it looks like you're referring to the recent searches that appear in the Google app search bar.

To clear them:

1. Open the **Google** app.
2. Tap your **profile picture** in the top-right.
3. Tap **Search history**.
4. You'll be taken to **My Activity**.
5. You can:
   - Tap **Delete** → **Delete today**, **Delete custom range**, **Delete all time**, or
   - Tap the **X** next to individual searches to remove them.

If you want to stop Google from saving searches in the future:

1. Go to **Profile picture** → **Search history**.
2. Turn off **Web & App Activity**, or enable **Auto-delete** (e.g., every 3, 18, or 36 months).

If you meant the suggestions that appear **before** you search (recent searches stored only on your phone), tap the search bar, then **press and hold** a search suggestion and choose **Delete** or **Remove**, if that option appears.

**ChatGPT:** For the **Google app on Android**, here's how to clear everything you can:

### Clear your Google search history
1. Open the **Google** app.
2. Tap your **profile picture** (top right).
3. Tap **Search history**.
4. Tap **Delete** → **Delete all time**.

### Clear the app's cache
1. Open **Settings** on your Android phone.
2. Tap **Apps** (or **Apps & notifications**).
3. Find and tap **Google**.
4. Tap **Storage** or **Storage & cache**.
5. Tap **Clear cache**.

### Reset the app completely (optional)
If you want to remove all local browsing data, suggestions, and cached content:
1. Go to **Settings → Apps → Google → Storage**.
2. Tap **Clear storage** (or **Clear data**).

This will:
- Clear the app's local data and cache.
- Sign you out of the Google app.
- Reset app preferences.
- **It will not delete your Google account or your online Search History** (that's done separately via **Search history → Delete**).

If your goal is **privacy before selling the phone or because you don't want previous searches showing up**, doing **both**:
1. **Delete all Search History**, and
2. **Clear Storage** for the Google app

will give you the cleanest reset.
