**ChatGPT:** Yes — **you can use top round beef (like Walmart’s milanesa-style thin cut)** for fried rice, and it actually works **very well** if you cook it correctly.

### Why it works
- **Thin cut** → cooks fast, perfect for stir-fry
- **Lean** → won’t make fried rice greasy
- Similar to beef used in **Chinese beef stir-fry** (just sliced differently)

### How to prep it so it’s not tough
Top round can get chewy if overcooked, so do this:

1. **Slice against the grain**  
   Even if it’s already thin, cut into **bite-size strips**.

2. **Quick marinade (10–15 min)**
   - 1 tbsp soy sauce  
   - 1 tsp cornstarch  
   - ½ tsp sugar  
   - ½ tsp oil (vegetable or sesame)

   👉 Cornstarch is key — it keeps the beef tender.

### Cooking method (important)
1. **Very hot pan or wok**
2. Add oil, then **beef first**
3. Sear fast — **30–60 seconds total**
4. Remove beef and set aside
5. Cook eggs, rice, veggies
6. Add beef back in **at the end** for 20–30 seconds

### Flavor ideas that pair great
- Garlic + green onion
- Soy sauce + oyster sauce
- A little MSG if you use it (you mentioned MSG before — solid move)
- Finish with sesame oil

## 🥩 Steak Melt (Top Round / Milanesa)

### Ingredients
- Top round milanesa beef (Walmart thin cut)
- Salt & black pepper
- Garlic powder (optional)
- Onion (yellow or sweet), sliced
- Butter
- Oil (veg or canola)
- Cheese: **American, provolone, or Swiss**
- Bread: **Texas toast, sourdough, or rye**
- Optional: mayo, Worcestershire

---

### Step-by-Step

#### 1️⃣ Prep the beef
- Cut beef into bite-size strips
- Season lightly with:
  - Salt
  - Pepper
  - Tiny pinch garlic powder
👉 Don’t over-season — cheese carries flavor

#### 2️⃣ Cook onions first
- Pan on medium
- Butter + oil
- Add onions + pinch salt
- Cook 8–10 min until soft & golden
- Remove and set aside

#### 3️⃣ Cook the steak (fast!)
- Same pan, **crank heat to high**
- Add oil
- Beef goes in **one layer**
- Cook **30–45 seconds**, flip, another **20–30 seconds**
- Splash Worcestershire (optional)
- Kill heat immediately

#### 4️⃣ Combine
- Add onions back to beef
- Toss once — done

---

### 🧀 Build the Melt
1. Butter outside of bread
2. Inside: steak + onions + cheese
3. Low-medium heat skillet
4. Cover pan to melt cheese
5. Flip once golden

---

## 🔥 Best Cheese Combos
- **American** → classic diner melt
- **Provolone + American** → creamy + stretch
- **Swiss + caramelized onions** → elite combo

---

## Pro Tips
- If beef feels stiff → **slice thinner**
- Want juicier meat? Add **½ tsp water or beef broth**, cover for 10 sec
- Mayo inside the bread = extra richness
- Don’t press the sandwich — keeps it juicy
