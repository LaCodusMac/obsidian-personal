**You:** Cheeseburger wrap

**ChatGPT:** A cheeseburger wrap is one of the best ways to use leftover burger patties.

## Ingredients
- 1 leftover burger patty
- 1 large carb wrap or flour tortilla
- 1–2 slices American or cheddar cheese
- Shredded lettuce
- Diced tomato
- Diced onion (raw or sautéed)
- Dill pickle slices
- Optional: bacon

### Burger Sauce
Mix together:
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tsp yellow mustard
- 1 tsp pickle relish (or finely chopped pickles)
- ¼ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp onion powder
- Black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Chop the leftover burger into bite-sized pieces.
2. Heat a skillet over medium heat. Add the burger with a splash of water or Worcestershire sauce and cook for 2–3 minutes until hot.
3. Lay the wrap flat and place the cheese in the center.
4. Add the hot burger on top of the cheese so it starts to melt.
5. Top with lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles, and a generous drizzle of burger sauce.
6. Fold in the sides, then roll it tightly like a burrito.
7. Place the seam-side down in the same skillet over medium heat for about 2–3 minutes per side until golden and crispy.

### Optional upgrades
- Add crispy bacon for a bacon cheeseburger wrap.
- Add sautéed mushrooms and Swiss cheese for a mushroom Swiss version.
- Add jalapeños and pepper jack cheese for a spicy burger wrap.
- Sprinkle everything bagel seasoning on the outside before toasting for extra flavor.

Serve with fries, potato wedges, chips, or a pickle spear. The crispy toasted wrap makes it taste much closer to a fresh cheeseburger than simply reheating the patty.
