**You:** General Tso chicken with sauce from bottle and frozen chicken

**ChatGPT:** Easy shortcut General Tso’s using frozen chicken and bottled sauce 👌

### Ingredients
- Frozen breaded chicken (nuggets, tenders, or popcorn)
- Bottled General Tso’s sauce
- Oil (optional, if pan-frying)
- Optional extras: garlic, ginger, red pepper flakes, green onions, sesame seeds

---

## Method 1 – Oven / Air Fryer (best texture)
1. Cook frozen chicken **exactly as package says** (air fryer or oven is ideal).
2. While cooking, warm the General Tso sauce in a pan on low.
   - Optional: add a little minced garlic or ginger.
3. Toss cooked chicken in sauce.
4. Top with green onions / sesame seeds.

---

## Method 2 – Stovetop (faster)
1. Pan-fry frozen chicken in a little oil until hot and crispy.
2. Lower heat, pour in sauce.
3. Toss for **1–2 minutes** until glazed.

---

## Pro Tips (makes it taste way better)
- Add **1 tbsp soy sauce + 1 tsp rice vinegar** to bottled sauce = more restaurant flavor.
- If sauce is too sweet: add **chili flakes or sriracha**.
- If too thick: splash of water.
- Serve over **white rice or fried rice**.

---

### Lazy upgrade (still easy)
Throw in some **steamed broccoli or bell peppers** at the end so it feels like real takeout.
