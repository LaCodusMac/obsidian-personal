### 1. Garlic Parmesan Chicken (My top pick)
Great with white rice.

**Ingredients**
- Chicken breast
- Butter
- Garlic powder
- Onion powder
- Paprika
- Salt & pepper
- Parmesan cheese

**How**
1. Butterfly or pound chicken to even thickness.
2. Season generously.
3. Cook in a skillet over medium-high heat for 5–7 minutes per side until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).
4. During the last minute, add a little butter, garlic powder, and Parmesan.
5. Serve over rice.

## Crispy Cheesy Chicken Wrap

### Ingredients
- 1 chicken breast
- 1 large tortilla or carb wrap
- Shredded cheddar, mozzarella, or a Mexican blend
- 1 tbsp oil or butter
- Salt
- Pepper
- Garlic powder
- Onion powder
- Paprika

**Optional add-ins**
- Bacon bits
- Buffalo sauce
- BBQ sauce
- Ranch
- Lettuce
- Tomato
- Onion
- Pickles

### Instructions

1. **Cook the chicken**
   - Butterfly the breast if it's thick.
   - Season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and paprika.
   - Cook in a skillet over medium heat for about **5–7 minutes per side**, until the thickest part reaches **165°F (74°C)**.
   - Let it rest for 5 minutes, then slice thinly.

2. **Assemble the wrap**
   - Sprinkle cheese on the tortilla.
   - Add the sliced chicken.
   - Add any extras (bacon bits, buffalo sauce, ranch, BBQ sauce, vegetables).
   - Add a little more cheese on top to help hold everything together.

3. **Fold**
   - Fold in the sides, then roll tightly from the bottom.

4. **Toast**
   - Place seam-side down in a lightly buttered or oiled skillet over medium-low heat.
   - Cook **2–3 minutes per side** until golden brown and the cheese is melted.

### Great flavor combinations
- **Buffalo:** Buffalo sauce + cheddar + ranch + bacon bits
- **BBQ:** BBQ sauce + cheddar + crispy onions (if you have them)
- **Chicken Bacon Ranch:** Ranch + bacon bits + cheddar
- **Garlic Parmesan:** Butter + garlic powder + Parmesan + mozzarella

Serve with fries, chips, or a side of rice if you want a bigger meal.

### Ingredients
- 1 chicken breast
- 1 large tortilla or carb wrap
- ½–1 cup shredded cheddar (or a cheddar/mozzarella blend)
- 2–3 tbsp buffalo sauce
- 1 tbsp butter or oil

**Optional**
- Bacon bits
- Ranch or blue cheese dressing
- Lettuce
- Diced tomato
- Red onion

### Instructions

1. **Season the chicken**
   - Salt
   - Pepper
   - Garlic powder
   - Onion powder
   - Paprika

2. **Cook**
   - Butterfly the chicken if it's thick.
   - Cook in a skillet over medium heat for **5–7 minutes per side**, until it reaches **165°F (74°C)**.
   - Rest for 5 minutes, then slice into strips.

3. **Buffalo it**
   - Toss the sliced chicken with **2–3 tablespoons of buffalo sauce** while it's still warm.

4. **Build the wrap**
   - Sprinkle cheese on the tortilla.
   - Add the buffalo chicken.
   - Add bacon bits if you have them.
   - Drizzle with ranch if you like.
   - Top with a little more cheese.

5. **Toast**
   - Fold the wrap tightly.
   - Cook seam-side down in a lightly buttered skillet over **medium-low heat** for **2–3 minutes per side**, until golden and crispy and the cheese is melted.

### Extra tip
For an even richer buffalo flavor, melt **1 tablespoon of butter** into the buffalo sauce before tossing it with the chicken. It makes the sauce cling better and gives it that classic wing flavor.

Pair it with fries, chips, or carrot and celery sticks with ranch for a restaurant-style meal.
