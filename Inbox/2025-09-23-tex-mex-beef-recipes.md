### 🌮 1. Tex-Mex Ground Beef Tacos
**Ingredients**  
- 1 lb ground beef  
- 1 packet taco seasoning (or chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper)  
- Taco shells (hard or soft)  
- Toppings: shredded cheese, lettuce, salsa, sour cream, avocado  

**Steps**  
1. Brown beef in skillet, drain excess fat.  
2. Stir in taco seasoning + ¼ cup water. Simmer until thickened.  
3. Serve in taco shells with toppings.  

---

### 🥘 2. Tex-Mex Skillet Rice
**Ingredients**  
- 1 lb ground beef  
- 1 onion, diced  
- 1 bell pepper, diced  
- 1 cup uncooked rice  
- 2 cups beef broth  
- 1 can diced tomatoes with green chilies (Rotel)  
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder, salt, pepper  
- 1 cup shredded cheese  

**Steps**  
1. Cook beef, onion, and bell pepper in skillet.  
2. Add rice, broth, tomatoes, and spices. Bring to boil.  
3. Reduce heat, cover, simmer 18–20 min until rice is tender.  
4. Top with cheese and let melt.  

---

### 🥙 3. Tex-Mex Beef Quesadillas
**Ingredients**  
- 1 lb ground beef  
- 1 small onion, diced  
- Taco seasoning  
- Flour tortillas  
- Shredded Mexican blend cheese  
- Salsa or sour cream for dipping  

**Steps**  
1. Cook beef + onion, season with taco spices.  
2. Place beef + cheese between two tortillas.  
3. Cook in skillet with a little butter/oil until crispy.  
4. Slice and serve with salsa or sour cream.  

---

### 🥣 4. Tex-Mex Chili
**Ingredients**  
- 1 lb ground beef  
- 1 can kidney beans  
- 1 can black beans  
- 1 can diced tomatoes  
- 1 small onion, diced  
- 2 tbsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika  
- Optional: corn, jalapeños  

**Steps**  
1. Brown beef with onion.  
2. Add beans, tomatoes, spices, and 1 cup beef broth.  
3. Simmer uncovered 25–30 minutes until thickened.  
4. Serve with shredded cheese, sour cream, and tortilla chips.  

---

### 🥔 5. Tex-Mex Beef & Potato Skillet
**Ingredients**  
- 1 lb ground beef  
- 3 medium potatoes, diced small  
- 1 onion, diced  
- 1 bell pepper, diced  
- 1 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, garlic powder  
- Shredded cheese & cilantro (for topping)  

**Steps**  
1. Fry potatoes in skillet until golden, set aside.  
2. Cook beef + onion + pepper, season well.  
3. Add potatoes back, stir together.  
4. Top with cheese & cilantro.

**ChatGPT:** Cooking ground beef is really straightforward—you just need a skillet, some fat (optional), and seasoning. Here’s a simple step-by-step guide:  

---

### 🔹 Basic Skillet Method
1. **Heat the pan**  
   - Place a skillet (nonstick or stainless) over medium-high heat.  
   - If your beef is lean (90/10 or leaner), add 1 tsp oil. If it’s fatty (80/20), you don’t need oil.  

2. **Add the beef**  
   - Break the raw ground beef into chunks in the pan.  

3. **Brown it**  
   - Let it cook for 2–3 minutes without stirring (helps develop flavor).  
   - Then break it apart with a spatula or wooden spoon.  

4. **Cook through**  
   - Stir occasionally until it’s fully browned and no pink remains (about 7–10 minutes, depending on quantity).  

5. **Drain (optional)**  
   - If you have a lot of grease, tilt the pan and spoon it out, or drain beef into a colander over a bowl.  

6. **Season**  
   - If making tacos, chili, or bowls—add spices (like chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, salt, pepper) once the beef is browned, then stir in ¼ cup water and simmer 2–3 min so seasoning coats the meat.  

---

### 🔹 Extra Tips
- **For crumbles** (like taco meat): break it up often with your spatula.  
- **For chunks** (like in pasta sauce): stir less so bigger pieces form.  
- **For extra flavor**: sauté onions, garlic, or peppers before adding beef.
