### 🥩 Pan-Seared Garlic Butter Sirloin Steak  

**Ingredients (for 2 steaks):**  
- 2 sirloin steaks (8–12 oz each, about 1–1.5" thick)  
- 2 tbsp olive oil (or avocado oil)  
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter  
- 3 garlic cloves (smashed, not minced)  
- 2–3 sprigs fresh thyme or rosemary  
- Salt & black pepper (generous)  

**Optional:** pinch of smoked paprika or steak seasoning blend  

---

**Instructions:**  
1. **Prep the steak** – Take out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking. Pat dry with paper towels. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper.  

2. **Heat the pan** – Use a cast iron skillet (best for a sear). Heat on medium-high until very hot. Add the oil and swirl.  

3. **Sear the steak** – Place the steaks in the pan (away from you). Let them sear **without moving** for 2–3 minutes until a golden crust forms. Flip and repeat.  

4. **Butter baste** – Lower heat to medium. Add butter, smashed garlic, and herbs to the pan. Tilt the skillet and spoon the melted butter over the steak continuously for 1–2 minutes.  

5. **Check doneness** – Use a meat thermometer:  
   - Rare: 120–125°F  
   - Medium Rare: 130–135°F  
   - Medium: 140–145°F  
   - Medium Well: 150°F+  

6. **Rest** – Transfer steak to a plate or cutting board and let rest for 5 minutes before slicing.  

7. **Serve** – Slice against the grain. Drizzle with pan butter and garlic.  

---

🔥 **Tips:**  
- For extra crust, pat the steaks dry right before searing.  
- If grilling, follow the same seasoning, then cook over high heat 3–4 min per side. Finish with garlic butter on top.  
- Great sides: roasted potatoes, sautéed mushrooms, or a simple arugula salad.
