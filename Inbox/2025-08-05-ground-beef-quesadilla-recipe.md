### 🔥 **Ground Beef Quesadilla**

**🕒 Total Time:** 25 mins  
**🍽️ Serves:** 2–3

---

### 🛒 Ingredients:

- 1/2 lb ground beef  
- 1/2 small onion, diced (optional)  
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder  
- 1/2 tsp cumin  
- 1/2 tsp chili powder  
- Salt & pepper to taste  
- 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, Monterey Jack, or a mix)  
- 2–3 large flour tortillas  
- Butter or oil for cooking  
- Optional extras:  
  - Jalapeños, cooked peppers, or black beans  
  - Sour cream, salsa, or guac for dipping

---

### 🔪 Instructions:

1. **Cook the beef:**  
   In a skillet over medium heat, cook the ground beef (and onion if using) until browned. Drain excess fat.

2. **Season:**  
   Add garlic powder, cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper. Stir and cook for another 1–2 minutes.

3. **Assemble quesadilla:**  
   Heat a clean skillet over medium. Lightly butter one side of a tortilla and place it butter-side-down in the pan.  
   On half of the tortilla, layer:
   - A handful of cheese  
   - A scoop of the beef mixture  
   - More cheese if you want it extra gooey  
   Fold over the other half.

4. **Cook:**  
   Cook 2–3 minutes per side or until golden brown and crispy, flipping once. Press down with a spatula to seal.

5. **Slice & serve:**  
   Cut into wedges and serve with salsa, sour cream, or guacamole.
