### Ingredients (2–3 wraps)
- 1 large chicken breast
- 2–3 large flour tortillas
- Buffalo sauce
- Ranch or blue cheese dressing
- Shredded lettuce
- Diced tomato
- Shredded cheddar or mozzarella
- Salt, pepper, garlic powder, paprika

### Easy Method
1. Slice chicken into strips or bite-sized pieces.
2. Season with:
   - salt
   - pepper
   - garlic powder
   - paprika
3. Cook in a skillet with oil over medium-high heat until browned and cooked through.
4. Toss chicken in buffalo sauce.
5. Warm tortillas slightly.
6. Layer:
   - ranch
   - lettuce
   - tomato
   - cheese
   - buffalo chicken
7. Wrap tightly and toast seam-side down in a pan for 1–2 minutes if you want it crispy.

- Add pepper jack cheese

- Add crushed tortilla chips inside for crunch

### If you want extra good flavor:
Marinate the chicken for 15–30 mins in:
- buffalo sauce
- a little pickle juice
- garlic powder

## Ingredients (Makes 3 Large Wraps)

### Chicken
- 1.25–1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- Optional:
  - pinch cayenne
  - pinch MSG for takeout-style flavor

### Sauce
- 1/2 cup buffalo sauce

### Wrap Fillings
- 3 burrito-size flour tortillas

# Step-by-Step

## 1. Prep the Chicken
Slice chicken breasts thinly or into bite-sized chunks.

Why:
- cooks faster
- better sauce coverage
- easier wrap bite

Pat dry first so it browns instead of steaming.

---

## 2. Season Aggressively
Mix seasonings directly onto chicken with olive oil.

You want the chicken to taste good BEFORE buffalo sauce goes on.

A weak seasoning base makes wraps taste flat.

---

## 3. Cook the Chicken
### Best method: Cast iron or stainless skillet
Medium-high heat.

Cook about:
- 4–5 mins first side
- 3–4 mins second side

You want:
- browned edges
- slight char
- internal temp 165°F

Do NOT constantly move the chicken around.

Let it sit and develop color.

---

## 4. Make Buffalo Sauce Better
In a bowl:
- buffalo sauce
- melted butter

Optional additions:
- tiny drizzle honey
- garlic powder
- splash Worcestershire

Toss cooked chicken immediately while hot.

---

# Wrap Assembly

## Layer Order Matters

### Correct Order
1. Sauce/dressing
2. Cheese
3. Chicken
4. Lettuce/tomato
5. Extra drizzle sauce

Putting lettuce under hot chicken makes it soggy fast.

---

## Cheese Choices
### Best Overall
- Sharp cheddar + mozzarella mix

### Spicier
- Pepper jack

### Restaurant-style
- Monterey jack

---

# How To Fold Properly
1. Fold sides inward
2. Pull bottom tightly over filling
3. Roll forward while compressing

Do NOT overfill.

Most homemade wraps fail because people stuff them too much.

---
