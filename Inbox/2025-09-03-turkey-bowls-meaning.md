**ChatGPT:** Here’s a simple **Korean-Style Ground Turkey Bowl** you can meal prep for the week 👇  

---

## 🍳 Ingredients (4 servings)
- 1 ½ lbs ground turkey (93% lean works best for flavor)  
- 3 cloves garlic, minced  
- 1-inch piece ginger, grated (or 1 tsp ground ginger)  
- 3 green onions, thinly sliced (save some for topping)  
- 2 tbsp sesame oil (or olive oil)  
- ¼ cup soy sauce (low sodium if preferred)  
- 2 tbsp brown sugar (or honey for lighter option)  
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar  
- 1–2 tsp gochujang (Korean chili paste) or sriracha (adjust heat)  
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)  
- 1 cup shredded carrots  
- 1 cup kimchi (optional, for topping)  
- 4 cups cooked white rice (or jasmine/brown rice)  
- 4 eggs (fried or soft-boiled, optional)  
- Sesame seeds (for garnish)  

---

## 🔥 Instructions
1. **Cook rice** → prepare your base first (white rice, jasmine, or brown).  
2. **Brown the turkey** → heat sesame oil in a pan, add ground turkey, garlic, and ginger. Cook until turkey is no longer pink.  
3. **Add sauce** → stir in soy sauce, brown sugar, rice vinegar, gochujang/sriracha, and red pepper flakes. Let it simmer for 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened.  
4. **Mix in veggies** → add carrots (lightly soften) and half the green onions.  
5. **Assemble bowls** → portion rice into containers, top with turkey mixture. Add kimchi, fried/boiled egg, remaining green onion, and sesame seeds.  

---

## 🥡 Meal Prep Tips
- Store in airtight containers (rice + turkey together, kimchi separate).  
- Keeps **4 days in the fridge**.  
- For reheating: microwave turkey + rice, then add kimchi, egg, and sesame seeds fresh.

## 🔥 Cooking Ground Turkey (for Korean Bowl)
1. **Heat your pan** → medium-high heat, add **1 tbsp sesame oil (or olive oil)**.  
2. **Add aromatics** → toss in **minced garlic + ginger** first, cook ~30 seconds until fragrant.  
3. **Cook the turkey** → crumble ground turkey into the pan, break it up with a spatula. Cook **5–7 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until no pink remains.  
   - Tip: don’t stir constantly — let it sear a little so you get some golden-brown bits (better flavor).  
4. **Drain if needed** → if you use higher-fat turkey, spoon out excess liquid/fat.  
5. **Add the sauce** → pour in soy sauce, rice vinegar, brown sugar, gochujang/sriracha, and red pepper flakes. Stir well.  
6. **Simmer** → reduce heat to medium-low and let it cook **3–4 minutes** so the turkey soaks up the sauce.  
7. **Finish** → stir in shredded carrots + half the green onions. Save the rest for garnish.
