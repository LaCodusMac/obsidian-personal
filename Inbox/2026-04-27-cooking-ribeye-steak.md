### 🧂 1. Prep (important)
- Take steak out **30–45 min before cooking**
- Pat it **VERY dry** (this is key for crust)
- Season generously with:
  - Salt (coarse if you have it)
  - Black pepper

---

### 🔥 2. Sear
- Heat a pan (cast iron ideally) on **high** until *very hot*
- Add a little oil (high smoke point like avocado or canola)
- Place steak down → **don’t touch it for ~2–3 min**
- Flip → another **2–3 min**

👉 You’re looking for a **dark brown crust**, not grey.

---

### 🧈 3. Baste (this is the upgrade move)
- Lower heat to medium
- Add:
  - 2 tbsp butter
  - Crushed garlic
  - Thyme (optional but 🔥)
- Tilt pan and **spoon butter over steak for ~1–2 min**

---

### 🌡️ 4. Doneness (pull temps)
- Rare: 120–125°F  
- Medium rare (best for ribeye): **130–135°F**  
- Medium: 140–145°F  

👉 If it’s thick, finish in oven at 400°F for a few minutes after sear.

---

### ⏱️ 5. Rest
- Let it rest **5–10 minutes**
- Don’t skip this — juices redistribute

---

### 🔪 6. Slice & Eat
- Slice against the grain
- Optional: sprinkle flaky salt on top

---

## ⚡ Quick Alternative (if you’re lazy)
- Salt + pepper
- Pan sear both sides ~3–4 min
- Done

…but honestly, with a $35 dry-aged ribeye, do the butter baste.

---

## 💡 Extra Tips (worth it)
- Dry-aged = already more intense flavor → **don’t over-season**
- No marinade needed
- If you have a thermometer, use it — makes it foolproof
