**You:** Ground beef quesadilla seasoning

**ChatGPT:** For a solid **ground beef quesadilla seasoning**, you want something bold but simple—basically taco-style.

### Easy Classic Mix (per 1 lb beef)
- 1 tsp chili powder  
- 1 tsp cumin  
- ½ tsp paprika (smoked if you have it)  
- ½ tsp garlic powder  
- ½ tsp onion powder  
- ½ tsp salt  
- ¼–½ tsp black pepper  
- Optional: pinch of cayenne or red pepper flakes for heat

### How to use it
1. Brown the beef, drain excess fat.  
2. Add seasoning + **2–3 tbsp water or beef broth**.  
3. Simmer 2–3 minutes so it coats and gets juicy.

### If you want it more “restaurant” flavored
Add one of these:
- Splash of **lime juice**
- ½ tsp **sugar** (balances spice)
- Little **Worcestershire** or soy sauce (umami cheat)

Perfect for quesadillas, tacos, burritos, nachos—basically anything Tex-Mex. 🌮
