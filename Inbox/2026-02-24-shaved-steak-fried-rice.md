You’ve been cooking shaved steak a lot lately — this is a perfect move. Quick, high protein, and hits hard.

### 🔥 Ingredients (2–3 servings)
- 1 lb **shaved steak**
- 3 cups **cold, day-old rice** (key for texture)
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup frozen peas & carrots (or whatever veg you’ve got)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2–3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp oyster sauce (optional but elite)
- ½ tsp MSG (optional but worth it)
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- Neutral oil (vegetable or avocado)
- Green onions (if you have them)

---

## 👨‍🍳 How To Make It

### 1️⃣ Prep First (Fast Cook)
- Break up rice so there are no clumps.
- Season shaved steak lightly with salt & pepper.

### 2️⃣ Cook the Steak (High Heat)
- Pan screaming hot.
- Oil → add steak in thin layer.
- Don’t move it for 30–45 sec so it browns.
- Cook fast (2–3 mins total).  
- Remove from pan and set aside.

### 3️⃣ Eggs
- Add a little more oil.
- Scramble eggs soft.
- Remove and set aside.

### 4️⃣ Aromatics
- Oil → garlic → 15 seconds.
- Add veggies and cook 1–2 mins.

### 5️⃣ Rice Time
- Add rice.
- Press into pan and let it sit to crisp slightly.
- Toss and repeat for some texture.

### 6️⃣ Sauce + Combine
- Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, MSG.
- Toss everything back in (steak + eggs).
- Finish with sesame oil.

---

## 🧂 Pro Tips (Level It Up)
- If rice is fresh and soft → spread on tray and refrigerate 30–60 mins first.
- Add a tiny splash of water before final toss if too dry.
- Want spicy? Chili oil or sriracha at the end.
- Want steakhouse vibes? Finish with a small pat of butter.
