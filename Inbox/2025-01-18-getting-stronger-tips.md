**ChatGPT:** To get stronger, you’ll want to focus on these key areas:

### 1. **Progressive Strength Training**
   - **Compound Lifts**: Focus on exercises like squats, deadlifts, bench press, overhead press, and pull-ups. These target multiple muscle groups and build overall strength.
   - **Progressive Overload**: Gradually increase the weight, reps, or intensity over time to challenge your muscles.
   - **Consistency**: Train each muscle group 2–3 times a week with sufficient rest between sessions.

### 2. **Nutrition**
   - **Protein**: Aim for 1.6–2.2 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight daily to support muscle growth.
   - **Calories**: Eat in a slight calorie surplus (more calories than you burn) if you're looking to gain muscle and strength.
   - **Carbohydrates and Fats**: Include enough carbs for energy and healthy fats for recovery and overall health.

### 3. **Rest and Recovery**
   - **Sleep**: Aim for 7–9 hours of quality sleep per night.
   - **Rest Days**: Incorporate rest days or active recovery to allow your muscles to repair and grow.

### 4. **Proper Form and Technique**
   - Work with a trainer or watch tutorials to ensure you perform exercises correctly. Proper form prevents injury and maximizes efficiency.

### 5. **Set Clear Goals**
   - Decide whether you're focusing on strength (e.g., lifting heavier weights) or hypertrophy (muscle size). Strength training typically involves lower reps with heavier weights, while hypertrophy uses moderate weights and reps (8–12).

### 6. **Supplement Wisely**
   - Consider supplements like **creatine**, **protein powder**, or **beta-alanine** if your diet isn’t meeting your needs.
