### 🏃 Typical Recovery Timeline

**Days 1–3**
- Full rest or light walking  
- Focus on sleep, hydration, and food  
- Your muscles are repairing micro-damage

**Days 4–7**
- Light runs (2–4 miles easy pace)  
- No speed work or long runs  
- If anything hurts sharply, keep resting

**Week 2**
- Return to normal easy training  
- Moderate runs are fine  
- Still avoid all-out efforts

**2–4 weeks after**
- Most runners can **race another half marathon safely**

### ⏱ Quick Rule of Thumb
- **Completion pace / easy effort:** ~2 weeks  
- **Hard race effort / PR attempt:** ~3–4 weeks  

### Signs You’re Ready Again
- Legs feel normal on easy runs  
- No lingering soreness in knees, hips, or Achilles  
- Your easy pace feels comfortable again

### Important for you (strength training note)
