### 🍝 **Tomato & Basil Pasta (for 2–3 servings)**  

**Ingredients**  
- 8 oz spaghetti (or penne/rigatoni)  
- 2 tbsp olive oil  
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced or minced  
- 1 can (14–15 oz) crushed tomatoes (or tomato puree)  
- 1 tsp sugar (optional, balances acidity)  
- 1 tsp dried oregano (or Italian seasoning)  
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional, for a little heat)  
- Salt & black pepper, to taste  
- 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, torn  
- Freshly grated parmesan cheese  

---

**Instructions**  
1. **Cook pasta** → Boil salted water, cook until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain.  
2. **Make sauce base** → In a skillet, heat olive oil over medium. Add garlic and cook until lightly golden (don’t burn).  
3. **Add tomatoes & seasonings** → Stir in crushed tomatoes, oregano, sugar (if using), red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper. Simmer 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally.  
4. **Toss pasta** → Add the cooked pasta directly into the skillet with sauce. If it’s too thick, splash in some of the reserved pasta water.  
5. **Finish** → Stir in fresh basil leaves right before serving. Top with parmesan.  

---

**How to Serve with Chicken Parm**  
- Plate the tomato & basil pasta first, then place the On-Cor chicken parm cutlet on top.  
- Spoon a little extra sauce over the chicken so it looks and tastes fresher.  
- Garnish with a basil leaf or extra parmesan for that restaurant look.
