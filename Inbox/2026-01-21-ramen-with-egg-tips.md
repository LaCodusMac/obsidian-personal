### Simple Ramen with Egg (3 ways)

#### 1. Egg Drop Style (silky)
1. Boil ramen with seasoning.
2. Beat 1 egg.
3. Slowly pour into boiling broth while stirring.
4. Let cook 30–60 seconds.

→ Makes the broth rich like egg flower soup.

#### 2. Poached Egg (runny yolk)
1. Boil ramen.
2. Crack egg straight into the pot.
3. Simmer 2–3 min (don’t stir).
4. Break yolk at the end.

→ Best if you want that yolk mixing into everything.

#### 3. Jammy Soft-Boiled (real ramen vibe)
1. Boil egg separately for **6½–7 min**.
2. Ice bath, peel, slice.
3. Add on top of finished ramen.

→ Restaurant-style.
