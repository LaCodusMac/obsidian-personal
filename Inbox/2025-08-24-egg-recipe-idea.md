### Garlic Butter Scrambled Eggs 🥚🧄  
**Ingredients (1–2 servings):**  
- 3 large eggs  
- 1 tbsp butter  
- 1 small garlic clove, minced (or ½ tsp garlic powder)  
- Salt & pepper to taste  
- Optional: chopped chives, shredded cheese, or hot sauce  

**Instructions:**  
1. Crack the eggs into a bowl, whisk with a pinch of salt and pepper.  
2. Heat a pan over medium-low heat, melt butter, and add garlic until fragrant (20–30 sec).  
3. Pour in eggs and gently stir with a spatula, pushing from the edges inward.  
4. Cook until just set but still creamy (about 2–3 min).  
5. Top with chives, cheese, or hot sauce if you like.  

🔥 Tip: Serve on buttered toast or wrap in a tortilla for a quick breakfast burrito.

### 🌯 Breakfast Burrito (1–2 servings)  

**Ingredients:**  
- 2 large eggs  
- 1 tbsp butter or oil  
- 1 medium tortilla (flour works best)  
- ½ cup shredded cheese (cheddar, pepper jack, or your fave)  
- 2 slices bacon, sausage, or diced ham (optional)  
- ¼ cup cooked potatoes or hash browns (optional but great)  
- Salsa, hot sauce, or avocado for topping  
- Salt & pepper to taste  

**Instructions:**  
1. **Cook protein** – In a pan, cook bacon/sausage/ham until browned, then set aside.  
2. **Scramble eggs** – In the same pan, melt butter, scramble eggs with salt & pepper until just set.  
3. **Warm tortilla** – Heat tortilla on the pan for 10–15 sec per side to make it flexible.  
4. **Assemble** – Layer eggs, protein, cheese, and potatoes in the tortilla. Add salsa or avocado.  
5. **Wrap & toast** – Fold burrito tightly. Place seam-side down in the pan for 1–2 min until golden and sealed.

- **Southwest:** Add black beans, corn, and jalapeños.

### 🌯 Veggie Breakfast Burrito  

**Ingredients (1–2 servings):**  
- 2 large eggs  
- 1 tbsp butter or olive oil  
- 1 medium tortilla (flour or whole wheat)  
- ½ cup shredded cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or pepper jack)  
- ½ cup cooked potatoes or crispy hash browns  
- ¼ cup bell peppers & onions (sautéed)  
- Salsa, hot sauce, or avocado  
- Salt & pepper to taste  

**Instructions:**  
1. **Cook veggies** – Sauté peppers and onions in oil/butter until soft and slightly charred.  
2. **Scramble eggs** – Add eggs, season with salt & pepper, cook until fluffy.  
3. **Warm tortilla** – Heat on a pan or directly over the burner for 10–15 sec per side.  
4. **Assemble** – Add potatoes, eggs, veggies, and cheese. Top with salsa or avocado.  
5. **Wrap & toast** – Fold burrito, then toast in pan (seam-side down first) until golden and sealed.

### 🥔 Air Fryer Hash Browns (shredded style)  

**Ingredients:**  
- 2 medium russet potatoes (or use frozen shredded hash browns)  
- 1 tbsp olive oil (or spray)  
- ½ tsp garlic powder (optional)  
- Salt & pepper to taste  

**Instructions (fresh potatoes):**  
1. **Prep potatoes** – Peel and shred potatoes with a grater.  
2. **Remove moisture** – Place shredded potatoes in a towel, squeeze out as much water as possible (important for crispiness).  
3. **Season** – Toss shreds with oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder if using.  
4. **Air fry** – Spread evenly in the basket (not too thick). Cook at **375°F (190°C) for 15–20 min**, flipping or shaking halfway through.  
5. **Finish** – Let rest 1–2 min for extra crisp before serving.  

---

### 🥔 If Using Frozen Hash Browns (shredded or patties):  
1. Spray lightly with oil.  
2. Air fry at **400°F (200°C) for 8–10 min** (shredded) or **10–12 min** (patties), flipping halfway.  

---

🔥 Pro tip: If you want them *extra crispy*, sprinkle a little cornstarch on the shreds before air frying.

**ChatGPT:** Yes — you *can* use tinfoil in an air fryer, but there are a few rules to do it safely:  

---

### ✅ When It’s Safe  
- Use **heavy-duty foil** (not thin flimsy sheets).  
- Place foil **only in the basket or tray**, never covering the entire bottom (airflow is key).  
- Poke a few small holes in the foil if you’re cooking something greasy, so air and fat can drain.  
- Keep foil **weighted down with food** so it doesn’t blow into the heating element.  

---

### ❌ What to Avoid  
- Don’t put foil in the very bottom of the fryer under the basket — it blocks airflow and can overheat.  
- Don’t use foil with acidic foods (like tomatoes, lemon, or vinegar marinades) — it reacts and gives a metallic taste.  
- Don’t let foil touch the heating element (fire risk).  

---

### 🔥 Pro Tip  
If you’re cooking something like **hash browns, veggies, or eggs in foil “packets”**, fold the edges up into a little tray. This keeps everything together and still allows hot air to circulate.
