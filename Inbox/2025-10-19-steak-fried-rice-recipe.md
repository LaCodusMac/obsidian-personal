### 🥩 **Steak Fried Rice**

#### **Ingredients (2–3 servings)**
- **8 oz steak** (sirloin, ribeye, or flank — sliced thinly against the grain)  
- **2 cups cooked rice** (preferably cold, day-old rice works best)  
- **2 eggs**, lightly beaten  
- **1 cup mixed vegetables** (peas, carrots, corn, or diced onion and bell pepper)  
- **2–3 cloves garlic**, minced  
- **2–3 tbsp soy sauce**  
- **1 tbsp oyster sauce** *(optional but adds depth)*  
- **1 tsp sesame oil**  
- **1–2 tbsp cooking oil** (vegetable or avocado oil)  
- **Salt and pepper** to taste  
- **Green onions**, chopped (for garnish)

---

#### **Instructions**

1. **Prep the steak**  
   - Slice the steak thinly and season with a bit of salt, pepper, and a splash of soy sauce.  
   - Optional: marinate for 10–15 minutes for more flavor.

2. **Cook the steak**  
   - Heat 1 tbsp oil in a hot wok or large skillet over **medium-high heat**.  
   - Add the steak and sear quickly (about 1–2 minutes per side) until browned but still juicy.  
   - Remove from the pan and set aside.

3. **Scramble the eggs**  
   - In the same pan, add a touch more oil if needed.  
   - Pour in beaten eggs and scramble quickly until just set.  
   - Remove and set aside with the steak.

4. **Stir-fry the vegetables**  
   - Add a little oil, then garlic and vegetables.  
   - Stir-fry for 1–2 minutes until fragrant and slightly tender.

5. **Add the rice**  
   - Break up clumps of rice and toss it into the pan.  
   - Stir-fry for 2–3 minutes, pressing the rice against the pan to get a little crisp on the edges.

6. **Season the rice**  
   - Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil. Mix thoroughly so everything is evenly coated.

7. **Combine everything**  
   - Add the steak and scrambled eggs back in.  
   - Toss everything together for another minute. Taste and adjust seasoning (add more soy sauce or pepper if needed).

8. **Finish**  
   - Turn off the heat, garnish with chopped green onions, and serve hot.

---

### 🔥 Tips
- Use **cold rice** for best texture — fresh rice can turn mushy.  
- Add **a dash of chili oil or sriracha** for spice.  
- For extra flavor, splash a bit of **rice vinegar** or **hoisin sauce** at the end.
