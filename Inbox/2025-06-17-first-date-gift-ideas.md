**ChatGPT:** Flowers are a classic and thoughtful first date gift, and pairing them with something small and meaningful can make an even better impression. Here are a few great ideas that go well with flowers:

### 🎁 **Small Gift Ideas to Pair with Flowers**
1. **A Box of Gourmet Chocolates**  
   – Timeless and indulgent. Go for quality over quantity—think dark chocolate truffles or artisan chocolates.

2. **A Handwritten Note or Card**  
   – Personal and meaningful. A short, sincere message shows thoughtfulness without being too intense.

3. **A Small Scented Candle**  
   – Choose a subtle, fresh scent like lavender, citrus, or vanilla. Avoid anything too overpowering.

4. **A Cute Bookmark or Small Book**  
   – If they enjoy reading, a pocket-sized book of poetry or short stories can feel personal and charming.

5. **Coffee or Tea Sampler**  
   – A few sachets of artisan tea or specialty coffee for a cozy touch.

6. **Macarons or Pastries**  
   – A small box of colorful macarons or a few elegant pastries can be visually impressive and tasty.

7. **Succulent or Mini Plant**  
   – A low-maintenance alternative to flowers that can last much longer.

8. **A Personalized Keychain or Trinket**  
   – Something small but meaningful if you’ve talked enough beforehand to tailor it to their interests.
