**You:** Does zoom have a 40 minute limit?

**ChatGPT:** Yes — **Zoom’s free (Basic) plan has a 40-minute limit**, but only in certain cases:

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 When the 40-minute limit applies
- **3 or more participants** in a meeting  
- The meeting will automatically end at ~40 minutes (you can restart it)

### 👥 When it does *NOT* apply
- **1-on-1 meetings** → no time limit on the free plan

### 💼 Paid plans (for context)
- Remove the 40-minute limit (meetings can run up to 30 hours)
- Require a licensed account (what your company is avoiding)

---

### What this means for your situation
- You **can’t reliably host a client presentation on free Zoom** if multiple people are involved  
- You’d risk getting cut off mid-meeting → not a good look
