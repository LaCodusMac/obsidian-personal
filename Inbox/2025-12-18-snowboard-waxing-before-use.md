**You:** Does a brand new snowboard need to be waxed or anything before you take it snowboarding?

**ChatGPT:** Short answer: **you *can* ride a brand-new snowboard straight out of the wrapper**, but **it’s usually better to wax it first**.

### Here’s why 👇

**Factory wax**
- New boards come with a **factory wax**, mainly to protect the base during shipping.
- It’s **thin and not very durable**.
- Fine for a few runs or a single day, but it wears off fast.

**Why waxing before riding helps**
- Better **speed and glide** (especially noticeable on flat run-outs).
- Helps the base **absorb wax properly** from day one.
- Extends the life of the base.
- Makes the board feel smoother and more predictable.

### Do you need anything else done?
- **Edges:** No tune needed. Factory edges are ready to ride.
- **Base structure:** Already done at the factory.
- **Detune:** Only if you’re riding park and feel catchy edges (most all-mountain boards are fine as-is).

### Best practice (ideal scenario)
1. **Hot wax** (temp-appropriate or all-temp)
2. Scrape + brush
3. Go ride

### If you don’t wax it first
Totally fine — just expect:
- Slightly slower board
- Wax will be gone quickly (after ~1 day)
