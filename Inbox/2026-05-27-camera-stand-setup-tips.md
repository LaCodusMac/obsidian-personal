For your Home Assistant / UniFi Protect setup, these stands are useful because the G4 Instants are awkward to place without mounts. Desk stands let you:
- angle them better for room coverage
- avoid adhesive wall mounting
- move them around while testing automations/zones
- place them on shelves near your Apple TV/HomePods/smart gear

A couple things to check when they arrive:
1. Stability  
Some 3D-printed stands wobble because the G4 Instant is back-heavy.

2. Cable routing  
Good stands leave enough room for the USB cable to bend naturally instead of stressing the connector.

3. Viewing angle  
You want enough tilt adjustment to avoid ceiling-heavy views. UniFi motion detection works much better when the camera sees horizontal movement across frame.

4. Print quality  
Cheap Etsy/eBay prints sometimes:
- crack around screw joints
- warp slightly
- use brittle PLA

If they feel flimsy, PETG prints hold up much better long term.

For ~$28 total for two, that’s within the normal range. Not a scam-level price and not overpriced either.
