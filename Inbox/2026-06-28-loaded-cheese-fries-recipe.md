### Ingredients
- Frozen fries (or homemade)
- 1–1½ cups shredded sharp cheddar (or cheddar/Monterey Jack blend)
- Salt and pepper
- Optional toppings:
  - Crumbled bacon
  - Green onions
  - Jalapeños
  - Sour cream
  - Ranch
  - Buffalo sauce

### Instructions
1. Bake or air fry the fries according to the package until **extra crispy**.
   - Oven: Add 3–5 extra minutes.
   - Air fryer: 400°F for 12–18 minutes, shaking halfway.

2. Spread the hot fries on an oven-safe tray or skillet.

3. Sprinkle the shredded cheese evenly over the fries.

4. Return to the oven at **400°F for 2–4 minutes**, just until the cheese is fully melted.

5. Add your toppings and serve immediately.

### Bacon Bit Cheese Fries

**Ingredients**
- Frozen fries
- 1–1½ cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 2–4 tbsp bacon bits
- Black pepper
- Garlic powder (optional)
- Green onions (optional)
- Ranch or sour cream for serving (optional)

### Instructions
1. Cook the fries until they're **golden and crispy**.
2. Spread them on an oven-safe baking sheet or dish.
3. Sprinkle the cheddar evenly over the fries.
4. Sprinkle the bacon bits over the cheese.
5. Bake at **400°F for 2–4 minutes**, just until the cheese is melted.
6. Finish with black pepper, green onions, and a drizzle of ranch or a dollop of sour cream if you have them.

If your bacon bits are the crunchy shelf-stable kind (like Bac'n Pieces), they're best sprinkled on **after** melting the cheese so they stay crunchy. If they're refrigerated real bacon pieces, you can put them on **before** the final bake so they warm through.
