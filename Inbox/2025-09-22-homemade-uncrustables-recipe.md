**You:** How to do homemade uncrustables

**ChatGPT:** Homemade Uncrustables are super easy and way cheaper than buying the frozen ones. Here’s how to make them:  

---

### Ingredients:
- Bread (soft sandwich bread works best)  
- Peanut butter (or any nut/seed butter)  
- Jelly/jam (or other filling: Nutella, honey, etc.)  
- Optional: ham, turkey, cheese for savory ones  

---

### Tools:
- A round cutter or cup (3–4 inches wide works well)  
- A fork (or an actual Uncrustables sealer if you have one)  

---

### Instructions:
1. **Spread filling**  
   - Spread a thin layer of peanut butter on one slice of bread (all the way to the edges—it helps seal).  
   - Add a little jelly in the center (not too much or it’ll leak).  
   - Top with the second slice of bread.  

2. **Cut & seal**  
   - Use a round cutter or glass to press out a circle.  
   - Seal the edges by pressing firmly with your fingers, or crimp with a fork.  
   - (If you want extra security, spread peanut butter around the edges before sealing—it acts like “glue.”)  

3. **Storage**  
   - Eat fresh, or wrap in plastic wrap/foil and freeze.  
   - If frozen, they last about 1 month. Just thaw for 30–60 minutes before eating.  

---

👉 Pro tip: For meal prep, make a batch, freeze them on a baking sheet, then bag them up so they don’t stick together.
