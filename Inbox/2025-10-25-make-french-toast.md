**You:** How to make French toast

**ChatGPT:** Here’s a simple, classic **French toast recipe** — crispy on the outside, soft and custardy inside:  

---

### 🧈 **Ingredients (for 2 servings / 4 slices)**
- 4 slices of **thick bread** (brioche, challah, or Texas toast are best)  
- 2 **large eggs**  
- ½ cup **milk** (whole milk or half-and-half for richness)  
- 1 tsp **vanilla extract**  
- ½ tsp **ground cinnamon** (optional but great)  
- Pinch of **salt**  
- **Butter** for frying  
- **Maple syrup**, powdered sugar, or fruit for serving  

---

### 🍳 **Instructions**
1. **Make the custard:**  
   In a bowl, whisk together eggs, milk, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt until smooth.  

2. **Dip the bread:**  
   - Heat a pan or griddle over **medium heat** and melt a bit of butter.  
   - Dip each slice of bread in the mixture for about **5–10 seconds per side** (enough to soak but not fall apart).  

3. **Cook:**  
   - Place soaked bread on the pan.  
   - Cook each side for **2–3 minutes**, or until golden brown and slightly crisp.  

4. **Serve:**  
   Stack on a plate and top with butter, maple syrup, powdered sugar, or fresh fruit.  

---

### 🔧 **Tips**
- **Stale bread** (a day old) absorbs the custard better and won’t get soggy.  
- For extra flavor, mix a little **nutmeg or brown sugar** into the custard.  
- For a richer texture, use **cream instead of milk**.
