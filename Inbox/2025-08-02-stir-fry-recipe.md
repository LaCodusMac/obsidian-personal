### 🥢 **Easy Stir Fry (Serves 2–3)**

#### **Ingredients**
**Protein (choose one):**
- 1 lb chicken breast (thinly sliced)  
- OR beef (sirloin or flank)  
- OR tofu (cubed)  
- OR shrimp  

**Veggies (mix and match):**
- 1 bell pepper (sliced)  
- 1 cup broccoli florets  
- 1 carrot (thinly sliced or julienned)  
- 1 zucchini or snap peas  
- ½ onion (sliced)  
- 2–3 garlic cloves (minced)  
- 1 tsp fresh ginger (grated or minced – optional)

**Stir Fry Sauce:**
- 3 tbsp soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce (or hoisin for sweet flavor)  
- 1 tbsp sesame oil (or any neutral oil for cooking)  
- 1 tsp cornstarch (to thicken)  
- ½ tsp sugar or honey  
- 2–3 tbsp water

---

#### **Instructions**

1. **Prep everything first.** Slice meat, chop veggies, and mix the sauce in a small bowl.
2. **Heat 1 tbsp oil** in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
3. **Cook the protein** until browned and cooked through. Remove from pan and set aside.
4. **Add more oil if needed** and stir-fry the veggies for 3–5 minutes, starting with harder ones (like carrots and broccoli).
5. **Add garlic and ginger** and stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
6. **Return the protein** to the pan, pour in the sauce, and toss everything together.
7. **Stir continuously** for 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats everything.
8. **Serve hot** over white rice, brown rice, or noodles. Top with sesame seeds or green onions if you have them.

### 🥢 **Beef and Broccoli Stir Fry** (Serves 2–3)

#### **Ingredients**

**For the Beef:**
- ¾ to 1 lb flank steak or sirloin, thinly sliced against the grain  
- 1 tbsp soy sauce  
- 1 tsp cornstarch  
- ½ tsp sesame oil (optional)

**For the Sauce:**
- 3 tbsp soy sauce  
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce  
- 1 tbsp brown sugar (or honey)  
- 1 tbsp cornstarch  
- 2–3 tbsp water  
- 1 tsp rice vinegar or mirin (optional)  
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger (or ¼ tsp ground)  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

**Veggies & Oil:**
- 2 cups broccoli florets  
- 1 tbsp oil (canola, vegetable, or avocado)

---

#### **Instructions**

1. **Marinate the beef:** In a bowl, combine sliced beef with 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp cornstarch, and sesame oil. Let it sit while prepping the rest (5–15 mins).

2. **Mix the sauce:** In a separate bowl, whisk together soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, cornstarch, water, vinegar, ginger, and garlic.

3. **Cook the broccoli:** Steam or microwave broccoli until just tender (about 2 mins), or stir-fry it with a splash of water in a hot pan, then remove.

4. **Sear the beef:** Heat oil in a skillet or wok over high heat. Add the beef in a single layer (do in batches if needed). Sear for 1–2 mins per side until just cooked, then remove from pan.

5. **Combine:** Reduce heat to medium. Add the sauce to the pan, stir constantly until it thickens (about 1–2 mins). Add beef and broccoli back in, toss to coat evenly.

6. **Serve:** Over white rice, jasmine rice, or noodles. Garnish with sesame seeds or scallions if you like.
