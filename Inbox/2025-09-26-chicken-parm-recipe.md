### 🍗 Ingredients (serves 4)  
- 2 large chicken breasts (butterflied & pounded to ½-inch thick, or use 4 thin cutlets)  
- 1 cup all-purpose flour  
- 2 large eggs (beaten with 1 tbsp water)  
- 1 ½ cups Italian-style breadcrumbs (or panko + Italian seasoning)  
- ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese  
- Salt & black pepper  
- 1 tsp garlic powder (optional, for breadcrumb mix)  
- 1 cup marinara sauce (homemade or good-quality jarred)  
- 1 ½ cups shredded mozzarella cheese  
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan (extra for topping)  
- Fresh basil (optional garnish)  
- Olive oil (for frying, about ½ cup)  

---

### 🔪 Directions  
1. **Prep the chicken**  
   - Season both sides with salt & pepper.  
   - Set up 3 bowls: flour → beaten eggs → breadcrumbs mixed with Parmesan & garlic powder.  
   - Dredge each cutlet in flour, dip in egg, then coat fully in breadcrumbs. Press breadcrumbs in so they stick.  

2. **Pan-fry cutlets**  
   - Heat ¼ inch of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high until shimmering.  
   - Fry chicken 3–4 minutes per side until golden brown and cooked through (internal temp 165°F).  
   - Drain on paper towels.  

3. **Assemble**  
   - Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).  
   - Spread a thin layer of marinara in a baking dish.  
   - Place chicken cutlets in dish, spoon sauce lightly over top (don’t drown them—keeps cutlets crisp).  
   - Sprinkle mozzarella and extra Parmesan on top.  

4. **Bake**  
   - Bake uncovered 12–15 minutes, until cheese is bubbly and slightly golden.  

5. **Serve**  
   - Garnish with fresh basil if you like.  
   - Serve with spaghetti, penne, or even garlic bread on the side.
