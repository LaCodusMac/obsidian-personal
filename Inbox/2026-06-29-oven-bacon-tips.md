### Ingredients

- Baking sheet
- Aluminum foil (optional, for easier cleanup)

### Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to **400°F (205°C)**.
2. Line a baking sheet with foil if desired.
3. If you have a wire rack, place it on the baking sheet and lay the bacon on the rack. If not, place the bacon directly on the sheet.
4. Arrange the slices in a single layer without overlapping.
5. Bake:
   - **Regular-cut:** 15–18 minutes
   - **Thick-cut:** 18–25 minutes
6. Start checking a few minutes before the expected finish time. Bacon goes from perfect to overdone quickly.
7. Transfer to a plate lined with paper towels and let it rest for 2–3 minutes—it will crisp up a little more as it cools.

**You:** Cleaning with a Clorox wipe while cooking after handling meat

**ChatGPT:** Yes, you can use **Clorox disinfecting wipes** while cooking, but use them carefully.

After handling raw meat:

1. Wash your hands thoroughly with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds.
2. Wipe down any contaminated surfaces (countertops, cabinet handles, faucet handles, etc.) with a Clorox wipe.
3. Make sure the surface stays **visibly wet for the contact time listed on the container** (many Clorox disinfecting wipes require around **4 minutes**, though some products vary).
4. If the surface is **food-contact** (cutting board, countertop where food will be placed, utensils, etc.), **rinse it with clean water after the required contact time** before preparing more food. This is because disinfecting wipe residue isn't meant to contact food.
5. Wash your hands again if they touch the wipe or any cleaning chemicals before returning to food preparation.

For knives, cutting boards, and utensils that touched raw meat, washing them with **hot, soapy water** (or in the dishwasher if dishwasher-safe) is generally the preferred method. You can disinfect afterward if desired, but it's important to clean first since disinfectants work best on already-clean surfaces.

A good workflow is:
- Handle raw meat → wash hands → wash knife/cutting board → disinfect nearby touched surfaces if needed → wash hands again → continue cooking.
